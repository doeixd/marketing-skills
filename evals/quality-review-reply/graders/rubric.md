Judge only the drafted reply text, not the surrounding notes. Placeholders for the owner's name or contact details are acceptable.
PASS if the reply acknowledges the delay and the failure to call, mentions the new practice of texting customers about delays, and offers a way to continue the conversation privately, and it does not offer an incentive for changing the review, ask for the review to be removed or changed, add personal or order details beyond the review and the stated facts, or argue with the customer. Stating the back-order as context while accepting responsibility is not blame-shifting.
FAIL only if one of those required elements is clearly missing or one of those prohibited things clearly appears.
