Use as an **adaptive interview and source checklist**, not a mandatory form for every user. Answer what is available, note unknowns, and only request the few missing inputs that change the decision.

## 3. Ask the owner (only where source evidence is insufficient)

- What should people reliably understand about the brand after one encounter?
- Why does the brand exist, and how has the product or customer experience changed over time?
- What would disappoint you if a marketer said it about the brand?
- What products or capabilities are genuinely stronger, and what evidence proves that?
- What common promises do competitors make that you intentionally do *not* make?
- What are the most important business outcomes for the next campaign or year?
- Which buyer occasions and customer types currently drive sales, versus those you hope to reach?
- Which current messages feel unmistakably on-brand? Which feel wrong, and why?
- What promises, words, policies and visual treatments require approval?

## 4. Ask customers / inspect customer language

- What prompted the search or purchase *that day*?
- What were you trying to achieve, feel, express or avoid?
- Which alternatives, including not buying, did you consider?
- What nearly stopped you? What ultimately made you comfortable?
- How would you describe the product and brand to a friend?
- Which part of the experience surprised, pleased or disappointed you?
- Where did you first encounter it? What do you remember?

Use real quotations only with provenance and permission; never invent customer voice.

## 5. Output checklist

- [ ] Claimed / expressed / perceived brand identity separated
- [ ] Category, competitive alternatives, parity and supported differences
- [ ] Feature→consequence→buyer benefit→proof→tradeoff map
- [ ] Distinctive brand cues and candidate buying occasions
- [ ] Stable voice profile and channel-specific tone variants
- [ ] Positioning, perspective, promise and goals with evidence status
- [ ] Core message, 3–5 pillars and permitted-claim register
- [ ] Reusable bullets, headlines, descriptions, Meta variants and other requested assets
- [ ] Brand-owner approval and a plan to validate uncertain hypotheses
