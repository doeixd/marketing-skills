# Creator-led ads, UGC, partnership ads, disclosures and permissions

Distinguish **organic creator content**, **paid partnership advertising**, **brand-produced UGC-style videos**, and **real independent customer endorsements**. Natural style is not evidence of authenticity, and creators are not interchangeable with testimonials.

## Before engaging a creator

- Verify genuine category fit, creator identity, brand safety, audience suitability and previous work. Don't infer sales from follower counts.
- Define deliverables by asset and placement (unboxing, try-on, demo, 9:16 videos, cutdowns, captions, thumbnails, still frames, raw masters).
- Define permitted claims and forbidden claims; provide verifiable product specs and actual terms.
- Agree usage rights: organic repost; paid ad usage; Meta partnership/whitelisting permissions; countries, duration, media, editing, exclusivity, approved audio, revocation, required takedown process, and compensation.
- Confirm product actually used/tested for any firsthand claim. Don't fabricate emotions, ownership, customer comments or unsolicited endorsements.

## Partnership-ad mechanics

A Meta **partnership ad** generally uses creator permission/identity to run approved creator content in Meta Ads Manager. Permission methods and eligible formats vary; verify active settings. No account/tool should attempt to reuse a creator's handle or content without explicit authorization.

**Paid relationship disclosure:** For U.S. audiences, FTC guidance treats payment, gifts, discounted products, employment or other material connections as requiring clear disclosure when material to endorsements. Platform paid-partnership labels **may not by themselves be sufficient**. Use hard-to-miss in-video and/or spoken disclosures appropriate to format, language, and viewing conditions. Avoid burying disclosure in hashtags or after `More`.

## Product/rights QA

| Asset | Confirm |
|---|---|
| Talent likeness/voice | Signed rights and permitted edit/AI usage |
| Creator handle, identity, account | Permission for partnership placements and schedule |
| Photo/video/master | Rights to upload, edit and use in paid media |
| Music/audio | **Paid advertising** license for territory/format; organic availability ≠ ad rights |
| Customer quote/review | Genuine, accurate, authorized, not selectively misleading |
| Claims on screen and voice | Actual product evidence; creator's experience is genuine |
| Packaging/unboxing | Real shipped contents, not an unavailable mockup |
| Disclosures | Clear, conspicuous, readable in safe zones, reviewed in each placement |

## Creative direction: guidance without scripting false personal testimony

Give the creator the *customer situation, desired clarity, proof to film, brand details and rights restrictions*. Leave their own diction and honest reaction intact. Don't force them to say they bought, loved, used or recommend the product unless true. Ask for raw footage as well as edited versions to create platform-specific crops without fabricating a testimonial.

## Risk triggers

No signed rights; unclear commercial music license; fake social-proof graphics; simulated testimonials presented as genuine; fabricated UGC; undisclosed financial connection; unauthorized synthetic clone; before/after claims requiring verification; use of current trends that require restricted rights. Block publication until corrected.
