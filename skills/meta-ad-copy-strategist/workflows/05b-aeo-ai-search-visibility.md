# Workflow 5B — AEO / AI-answer visibility → source quality → citation measurement

**Trigger:** User asks about appearing in Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Bing Copilot, AI answers/citations, AI-generated recommendation visibility, AEO/GEO/LLMO strategy or brand misinformation in generated answers.

**Position:** Optional extension of [SEO 5A](05a-seo-and-organic-search.md), inside Stage 5. Read [AEO reference](../references/aeo-generative-search-and-citations.md), [SEO reference](../references/seo-fundamentals-and-ecommerce.md), [Brand Discovery 3A](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), and [Offering Discovery 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md). Use [Search & Conversion Audit](../templates/search-and-conversion-audit.md).

**Primary principle:** AEO is not a secret ranking trick. As of Google's 2026 guidance, **SEO fundamentals are also the fundamentals for Google AI Search**; there is no extra technical requirement, no special AI schema, and no `llms.txt` requirement. AI answers vary by query, time, locale and system, and no third party can guarantee a mention or citation.

## 0. Define the desired observable outcome
- Clarify *which engine/surface* (Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Bing/Copilot, another assistant, agentic shopping), market, language, topic/brand/products, and commercial objective.
- Distinguish **being accurate/recognizable**, **being cited**, **receiving a referral**, and **converting a buyer**. They are different outcomes.
- Never claim access to a platform's internal answer ranking; specify tool/report visibility limitations.

## 1. Build a source-of-truth and question map
- Use verified product catalog, service facts, founders/company details, shipping/returns and approved claims, not speculative marketing adjectives.
- Derive real questions from buyer research and search queries: what is it, which is suitable, how does it work, what does it cost, what are drawbacks, which alternative, how to use, can I trust it?
- Map `question → buyer/intent → authoritative page → specific verified answer → source/proof → update cadence → next action`.
- Inspect current pages for first-hand non-commodity utility, accurate dates, honest comparison and obvious missing details.
- Review entity names and product identifiers for ambiguity and consistency, without inventing external reviews, profiles or citations.

## 2. Confirm technical eligibility through SEO
- Check crawl, index, canonical and snippet-eligibility requirements before speculative AEO tweaks, as supported by [Google AI Features guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).
- Make important text visible, internally discoverable and accessible; ensure structured data matches the visible source and is *supported* for the relevant search feature.
- For products, align correct feed/merchant details with on-page facts; for services, publish actual scope, process and evidence.
- Do **not** require special AI-readable files, artificial section chunking or arbitrary FAQ schema for AI visibility.

## 3. Improve the actual answer resource
For each high-value question, produce or improve material with:
- A direct, human-friendly answer in context, clear definitions and concrete relevant details.
- Original field evidence, product photography, real measurements, demonstrations, transparent methods, useful examples and limitations.
- Real comparative context where applicable; do not misrepresent competitors.
- Visible source, authorship and update/verification date when relevant.
- Plain navigation and next appropriate action, not an aggressive sales pitch on every educational page.
- A source-linked claim register and maintainer for changing facts.

Publish only content that would be useful even if no search assistant ever cited it. Mass-producing thin "AI optimized" FAQ pages is inappropriate.

## 4. Measure visibility without pretending to control answers
- GSC: review relevant query/page search impressions/clicks and generative AI reports **where account feature is available**, respecting report coverage. Do not invent a report's historical data.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: use [AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c) for cited pages, aggregated grounding queries and citation trends. **Citations ≠ clicks, rankings or attributable revenue**.
- For other assistants where no reliable report exists: label manual sampled prompts as exploratory. Log engine/version, query, locale, date, browsing mode, citation visibility and competitor context. Don't declare AI "share of voice" from cherry-picked prompts.
- Connect referral visits and qualified outcomes where measurable, with consent and attribution limitations. Avoid claiming causation from a citation uptick alone.

## 5. Decision and handoff
- If page not eligible/indexed → [SEO 5A](05a-seo-and-organic-search.md).
- If facts wrong/incomplete → [Offering 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) and content-source fix.
- If brand unknown or inconsistent → [Brand 3A](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md).
- If traffic arrives but doesn't convert → [CRO 4A](04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md).
- If citations rise but commercial results don't → review intent and investment; don't report a victory without buyer value.

**Output:** question/answer evidence map, prioritized factual/content/technical fixes, approved page briefs, AI citation monitoring plan and explicit uncertainty log. Rank work by real buyer relevance, source originality, technical eligibility, feasibility and estimated business value. No citation guarantees.
