# Worked example: ring product from creative hypothesis to Reels script

**Fictional example, not an actual brand or ad result.** Assume a seller has verified a collection of vintage-inspired rings. Any unverified price, gift packaging, reviews, material, origin, warranty, promotion, fit/comfort or shipping claim stays absent. Product visuals must be actual stocked items, not generated false specimens.

## Strategic angle 1: tactile product detail

- **Buyer situation (hypothesis):** Comparing aesthetically interesting rings online; uncertain what the pattern looks like up close.
- **Promise:** The design details are worth examining; not a claim that the materials are premium or handmade.
- **Proof:** Authorized macro and turning shots of the *real* SKU. Photo/video shows both front and side; include true scale cue.
- **Brand cue:** Actual wordmark in first composition or authentic product presentation; never invent packaging.
- **Opening shot:** 3/4 macro of ring slowly rotated in hand; real reflections preserve true metal color.
- **Visual hierarchy:** ring occupies substantial frame; one simple overlay, no fake discount sticker.

### Illustrative 15-second storyboard

| Time | Visual | Voice / sound | On-screen words | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Close-up of the real ring on finger or display, brand cue legible | Natural product sound or cleared ambient track | `Look at the detailing` | Product-specific hook and brand |
| 2–6 | Slow true-color turn, side profile visible | `Vintage-inspired rings with a closer look at every curve.` | `Vintage-inspired design` | Actual attribute, no material claim |
| 6–11 | Two actual angles/variants with accurate size context | `Explore the shapes and find the one you like.` | `See the full collection` | Choice and scale |
| 11–15 | Clean hero SKU + correct brand name and website handoff | `Browse the collection.` | `Explore the rings` | Action and branding |

**Ad fields:**
- Primary text: `Vintage-inspired rings with details worth a closer look. Explore the designs.`
- Headline: `Explore Vintage-Style Rings`
- Description (optional): `See the collection`
- CTA: choose a valid shop-oriented CTA in the actual ad setup.
- Destination: real collection page, confirmed to show these SKUs and verified terms; URL intentionally omitted.

## Strategic angle 2: gift decision (different *motivation*)

- Buyer (hypothesis): Someone seeking a visually distinctive gift, worried about choosing the right style.
- Visual: Two real variants on an uncluttered surface; show differences honestly. If gift packaging is actually offered, document and film it; otherwise **do not show it**.
- Hook: `Choosing a ring as a gift?` (non-sensitive and non-assumptive); follow with options and how to inspect style/sizing.
- Needed real-world fix: if product pages lack sizes, improve them before claiming gift-friendly certainty.
- Test question: Does gift/occasion communication attract *more qualified purchases* than aesthetic detail, when both use equivalent budgets/landing pages? Don't claim an answer in advance.

## Placement cuts

- **Instagram Feed 4:5:** use macro hero, one small overlay; allow primary text/headline to supply context; verify actual preview.
- **Instagram Reels 9:16:** shot movement, brand cue and in-frame words; keep key text in current safe zone, subtitles legible and muted story understandable.
- **Instagram Story 9:16:** use 1–2 distinct beats; avoid long text, don't assume feed headline appears.
- **Carousel:** card 1 hero/style, card 2 detail, card 3 honest variant options, final exploration. First card still identifies the product/brand.
