# Meta / Instagram creative placement playbook (reviewed 2026-11-06)

**Scope:** Facebook and Instagram paid-ad creative. Platform rules and available formats change; check [Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide), [Advertising Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/) and *actual account previews* before spending. Some official pages require sign-in, and Meta's different documents sometimes disagree; never silently call an advisory figure a hard cap.

## Production masters: useful defaults, not one universal export rule

| Placement/format | Creative master | Creative language | Preview checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Facebook Feed single image | 4:5 portrait, often 1440×1800 px; 1:1 also supported | Hero/detail/lifestyle or legible promotional artwork | Crop, first visible primary-text line, headline/description appearance |
| Feed video | 4:5 or 1:1 where appropriate; source high resolution | Product clear early, meaningful motion, captions | thumbnail, first seconds, muted comprehension |
| Stories | 9:16 portrait, often 1440×2560 px (1080×1920 also common/accepted) | Full-screen single-beat story, concise in-frame message, audio when useful | UI top/bottom, CTA overlay, link/offer context |
| Instagram/Facebook Reels | 9:16 vertical video, often 1440×2560 px | Native-feeling hook/demo/narration, sound-on design plus captions | Reels UI/right rail, caption overlay, first-frame legibility, sound license |
| Carousel | Usually 1:1 card masters; verify exact chosen placement | First card earns swipe; sequence solves one decision | card order, individual destinations, consistent crop, headline per card |
| Collection / catalog | Feed/format-specific product feed and cover | Shoppable catalog relevance; variant/photo accuracy | availability, price, price overlays, mobile storefront |

**Safe zone guidance:** For Reels/Stories 9:16 creative, a conservative cross-placement *starting template* leaves approximately **14% top, 35% bottom, 6% each side** clear of *essential* text/logo; vary by product and placement because Stories often has less bottom obstruction and Meta's documents differ. These percentages are not a guarantee; use Ads Manager previews. At 1080×1920, 14%≈269 px, 35%≈672 px, 6%≈65 px. Feed 4:5 does **not** share a universal official percentage-based safe zone. If disclaimer is needed, protect still more lower space. Keep real action, proof, and key text in visible zones, not necessarily all decorative art.

**Field-level behavior:** Feed commonly displays *primary text above the creative*, headline *below the creative*, and description only on some placements; in Reels/Stories, text can be less visible, and the video must carry the core message. An approximately 125-character primary text and ~40-character headline are writing heuristics for common feeds, **not hard universal maximums**. Exact recommended character counts differ by placement; headline and description can be truncated or omitted. Avoid placing time-limited essential terms solely in description or captions.

**Media:** JPG/PNG common for stills; MP4/MOV for video. 1080-wide exports are widely used even where higher Meta recommendations are given. Check duration/file-type/size in the current Ads Guide and interface: published official pages have sometimes differed on durations. For Reels, short is often a useful creative starting point—not a forced Meta maximum or universal optimum.

## Placement-specific creative grammar

### Feed
- A still can carry the pitch instantly. A video should work from its thumbnail and first frames.
- Expect a mix of silent and sound-on consumption; visible captions and action must convey meaning even muted.
- Avoid the assumption that viewers expand `See more` or that the description renders.
- Crop deliberately: the same image cropped from 9:16 can lose the product or text in 4:5.

### Stories
- One visual beat per frame, simple directional movement, optional sequence of 2–3 frames with a clear progression.
- Put the persuasive fact *in the creative*, since feed-style headline placement may differ.
- Don't place overlays where account name, controls or CTA will cover them.

### Reels
- Build *for 9:16 from the outset* where feasible. A square video with filler bars is usually a weak adaptation.
- Hook with a relevant product moment, demonstration, interesting detail, or buyer question; show the product and an attributable brand cue early without an unnecessary logo delay.
- Audio helps a Reels-first design, but captions/visual demonstration are still needed for muted viewing and accessibility.
- Use native-feeling pacing and scenes; avoid copying creator slang or trending formats if the brand voice doesn't fit.
- Verify current music, effect, asset and partnership permissions. A track available for an organic Reel is **not automatically cleared for paid advertising**.

### Carousel
- Card 1: value/curiosity relevant to category. Cards 2–N: sequential explanation (features, design variety, real alternatives, gift/size guidance). Final card: truthful action/destination.
- Don't depend on users reaching the final card to learn what is being sold.
- In each card, keep headline and product reference correct; avoid mismatched variants/prices. Check whether card ordering may be optimized automatically in the chosen setup.

## Meta's automated creative transformations

Advantage+ creative may modify presentation or generate variants (text improvements, overlays, media expansion, automatic music, subtitles, translation, relevant comments, background generation, product tags). Available toggles vary by ad format and account. Track per creative:

`enhancement | originally requested | enabled/disabled | changes to SAY / SHOW / SEND | preview_checked | owner_approved`.

**SAY** = factual message, price, offer, claim, translation; **SHOW** = generated pixels, product depiction, overlays; **SEND** = CTA or destination. For tightly controlled/regulated claims, do not accept unreviewed SAY/SHOW/SEND changes. Recheck after cloning or editing ads; compare the final rendered variants to the approved source. See [creative QA](creative-quality-control.md).
