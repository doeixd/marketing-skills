# Marketing effectiveness: immediate sales, future demand and causal evidence

**Use when:** choosing campaign objectives and metrics; interpreting results; advising on short-term activation versus brand memory; designing creative experiments.

## Separate objectives and time horizons

Binet and Field's effectiveness work emphasizes both **long-term brand building** and **short-term sales activation**. A campaign can contain both functions, but they may require different message, audience and measurement plans.

| Marketing function | Primary job | Illustrative creative | Appropriate measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immediate activation | Help buyers currently considering the category choose and act | Clear product, offer, benefit, proof, CTA | Incremental sales, CPA, contribution margin, qualified leads |
| Brand memory / future demand | Increase probability the brand is recognized or retrieved at buying occasions | Brand plus distinctive assets and relevant category entry points | Recognized/attributed recall, brand associations, reach and longer-run incrementality |
| Hybrid | Build a recognizable association while making buying simple | Clear branded product benefit and occasion | Both, tracked separately |

The widely repeated 60:40 brand-to-activation ratio is an aggregate illustration, **not** a mandatory budget allocation for every business, market or ad account. Do not assume brand effects from a nice-looking ad without measurement.

## How to test messages without confusing correlation with causation

1. **State hypothesis.** "Gifting copy improves incremental purchases versus design copy because [specific buyer motivation]."
2. **Select experiment unit.** Copy-only test, complete concept (copy + visual), offer test, landing-page test, or brand/creative test. Know which causal claim each can support.
3. **Choose main KPI in advance.** For ecommerce: incremental contribution or purchases; for lead-gen: qualified lead economics; for brand: appropriate recall, consideration or association survey measures.
4. **Track guardrails.** CTR, CPC, landing-page views, refunds, negative feedback, short-term sales and margin can explain results but should not silently replace the primary objective.
5. **Control major confounds where feasible.** Audience overlap, budget, seasonal demand, delivery optimization, promotion, price, inventory, creatives, attribution setting, device and landing page.
6. **Interpret uncertainty.** Do not make significance claims without suitable design, sample size and statistical analysis. Meta may allocate impressions unevenly even across ads within an ad set.
7. **Update the buyer and brand hypotheses.** Keep negative, inconclusive and contradictory results. Avoid attributing improvement to copy when image and price also changed.

## The relationship between creative diversity and brand consistency

Creative concepts should vary **why buy** (desired progress, buying occasion, product value, risk reduction, identity, trust) while preserving appropriate brand cues. Brand asset recognition and distinct positioning hypotheses are not competing objectives.

- **Poor diversity:** fifteen synonymous headlines, all targeting the same desire.
- **Better diversity:** gifting, personal style, product specifics and credible risk reduction as separate hypotheses with corresponding images.
- **Brand consistency:** coherent logo/product presentation, identity and truthful market category across all.

## Evidence and measurement cautions

- Meta Ad Library can reveal an active commercial ad and its wording; it generally does not reveal that ad's ROAS, purchases or profit.
- CTR, engagement and CPC are diagnostics, not proof that customers value the product more.
- ROAS is incomplete without margins, organic sales cannibalization and attribution limitations.
- Brand measurement needs suitably designed survey, lift or longitudinal evidence, not an AI's subjective "memorability score."
- Avoid applying B2B '95–5' demand ratios as literal facts to every ecommerce category.
- If evidence volume is low, report a tentative pattern and recommend an informative next step rather than declaring a winner.

## Suggested learning record

`date_range | offer | audience | placement | buyer_situation | category_entry_point | position | brand_cues | concept_angle | claim_evidence_ids | copy_version | visual_version | landing_page | spend | impressions | outbound_clicks | landing_page_views | purchases | net_revenue | contribution_margin | recall_metric_if_valid | attribution | confounds | interpretation | confidence | next_test`

Never fill unknown metrics with invented zeros or proxy estimates. Preserve provenance.
