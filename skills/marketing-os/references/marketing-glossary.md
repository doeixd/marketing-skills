# Marketing glossary

Plain definitions of terms used across Marketing OS, with the distinctions that cause the most confusion. Exact formulas for a given business belong in its [KPI dictionary](../templates/marketing-measurement-and-kpi-dictionary.md).

## Money and efficiency

| Term | Meaning | Don't confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| **Contribution margin** | Revenue minus the variable costs of that sale (product, shipping, fees, discounts, returns), before marketing | Gross margin, which usually omits shipping, fees and returns |
| **CAC** (customer acquisition cost) | Marketing and sales cost ÷ **new customers** acquired | **CPA** (cost per action), the cost of any chosen action: a lead, an order, a signup. A repeat order has a CPA but no CAC |
| **Allowable CAC** | The most you can pay for a customer and still meet your profit or payback goal | Actual CAC |
| **ROAS** (return on ad spend) | Revenue attributed to ads ÷ ad spend, usually as reported by one platform | **MER** / blended ROAS: *total* revenue ÷ *total* marketing spend, which no platform can inflate |
| **Break-even ROAS** | 1 ÷ contribution margin %. At 40% margin, ads break even at 2.5 | Target ROAS, which adds your profit requirement |
| **ACOS / TACOS** | Amazon terms. ACOS = ad spend ÷ ad-attributed sales; TACOS = ad spend ÷ total sales | Each other: TACOS shows whether ads grow the whole business |
| **CLV / LTV** (customer lifetime value) | Contribution a customer generates over a stated period | Revenue-based LTV with no horizon, which overstates what you can spend |
| **CAC payback** | Months of contribution needed to recover CAC | LTV:CAC ratio, which ignores how long cash is tied up |
| **AOV** | Average order value | Revenue per visitor (AOV × conversion rate) |
| **MRR / ARR, NRR** | Monthly/annual recurring revenue; net revenue retention = recurring revenue from last period's customers now, including upgrades, downgrades and churn | Logo retention (share of customers kept) |

## Measurement

| Term | Meaning | Don't confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| **Attribution** | A rule for assigning credit for a conversion to touchpoints | **Incrementality**: sales that happened *because of* the marketing, found by experiment |
| **Attribution window** | How long after a click or view a platform claims a conversion | Time to purchase |
| **View-through conversion** | Conversion credited after someone saw but didn't click an ad | Click-through conversion |
| **Holdout / lift test / geo test** | Withholding marketing from a comparable group or region to measure its real effect | A/B test of two creatives, which measures relative, not absolute, effect |
| **MMM** (marketing mix modeling) | Statistical estimate of each channel's contribution from aggregate data over time | Multi-touch attribution, which uses user-level paths |
| **Conversion rate** | Conversions ÷ a stated denominator (sessions, users, clicks) | A rate with an unstated denominator |
| **Pixel / tag** | Browser code that reports events to a platform | **Conversions API / server-side tracking**, which sends events from your server |
| **Deduplication** | Matching the browser and server copies of one event by a shared event ID so it counts once | Removing duplicate customers |
| **Event match quality** | How well the customer details sent with an event let the platform match it to a user | Data accuracy |
| **UTM parameters** | Tags on a URL naming source, medium and campaign for analytics | Click IDs (gclid, fbclid) added by platforms |
| **Learning phase** | The period when an ad platform is still exploring who to show an ad set to; usually ends after about 50 optimization events in a week | A fixed number of days |
| **Optimization event** | The action a campaign is told to maximize | Campaign objective, the broader category |

## Strategy

| Term | Meaning | Don't confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| **Positioning** | The value an offering provides to a defined buyer against their real alternatives | Tagline or brand identity |
| **JTBD** (jobs to be done) | The progress a buyer is trying to make in a situation | Persona demographics |
| **Category entry point** | A situation or need that brings the category to mind | Keyword |
| **Awareness levels** | Unaware → problem-aware → solution-aware → product-aware → most aware; a lens for choosing a message | A fact known about an individual |
| **ICP** (ideal customer profile) | The kind of account or customer that buys readily and stays | Total addressable market |
| **MQL / SQL / PQL** | Marketing-qualified, sales-qualified, product-qualified lead, as defined in writing by that business | Each other, or a raw lead |
| **ABM** | Account-based marketing: marketing to a named list of companies | Lead generation to anyone who fits |
| **Brand vs activation** | Building future demand and memory vs converting existing demand now | Organic vs paid |
| **PESO** | Paid, earned, shared, owned media | A funnel |
| **VOC** | Voice of customer: buyers' own words from reviews, interviews and support | Survey scores |

## Channels and assets

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **PDP** | Product detail page |
| **RSA** | Responsive search ad (Google) |
| **Custom audience / lookalike** | A list of your own contacts or visitors on an ad platform / people the platform judges similar to them |
| **Advantage+, Performance Max, Smart+, Performance+** | Meta, Google, TikTok and Pinterest automated campaign types that choose audience, placement and creative combinations for you |
| **Spark Ads / partnership ads** | Running a creator's or your own organic post as an ad, with authorization |
| **UGC** | User-generated content; needs permission for each use |
| **SEO / AEO (GEO)** | Being found in search results / being accurately cited in AI-generated answers |
| **CRO** | Conversion rate optimization: improving the share of qualified visitors who complete a valuable action |
| **ESP / CDP / CRM** | Email service provider / customer data platform / customer relationship management system |
| **Reverse ETL** | Sending warehouse data back out to marketing tools and ad platforms |
| **Dunning** | The process of recovering failed subscription payments |
| **GBP** | Google Business Profile |
| **GPC** | Global Privacy Control, a browser opt-out signal ([privacy reference](privacy-consent-and-data-protection.md)) |
