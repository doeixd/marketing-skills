# Shopify promotion economics, bundles, seasonal merchandising and commerce campaign calendars

Reviewed 2026-11-06. Use [Ecommerce Promotion Workflow 5J](../workflows/05j-shopify-ecommerce-promotions.md), [campaign template](../templates/shopify-ecommerce-promotion-brief.md), [Store Diagnosis 1B](../workflows/01b-shopify-ecommerce-diagnosis.md) and [Promotion/PESO 5C](../workflows/05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md).

## A promotion is a commercial offer, not just a banner

Decide **why someone should buy this offering now**, whether the promotion satisfies an actual buying occasion, and whether increased sales create **incremental contribution**. Non-discount strategies include genuinely curated collections, product demonstrations, helpful gift guides, new assortment with verified differences, real creator collaborations, education, useful bundles and honest shipping confidence. Avoid price promotions as a default.

Real levers: absolute/percentage product or order discount; eligible free shipping; true bundle/set pricing; quantity break; gift-with-purchase (real stock); new SKU launch; customer appreciation; preorders (only with actual terms); seasonal occasion; targeted segment offer. Reject fake regular prices, repeated "last chance" for a rolling deal, false limited inventory or unsubstantiated promises.

## Shopify discount configuration and mechanics

Use Shopify's real **Discounts** system, plan/eligible market, code vs automatic discount, active period, one-time limits, customer eligibility and product exclusions. **Combinability is not automatic:** product/order/shipping discount classes have specific rules and merchant eligibility. Shopify Plus has additional same-line-item discount capabilities. Inspect current store configuration and all overlapping offers, carts and shipping rates before setting up discounts. Test discount amount/order subtotal/taxes, stacking, cart messages and postpurchase behavior. Current 2026 Shopify automatic discount limits and combinations must be checked in official docs.

**Shopify Bundles**: inventory, sales channel support, price changes and discount applications are not always intuitive. In particular, Shopify documentation says bundle prices **do not automatically update** when component prices change, and Search & Discovery bundle filtering can have limitations. Verify component stock, fixed/other bundle types, actual checkout eligibility, expected package/fulfillment and break-even margin. Do not claim a bundle "saves" money unless buyer-visible comparison is true and compliant.

## Merchandising and seasonal sequencing

Plan actual buyer occasions (gifts, holiday, events, replenishment, school/work cycles, local demand) with inventory and shipping lead times rather than a generic 12-month promotion schedule. A gifting campaign needs verified delivery cutoff, size/fit support, return/exchange policy, packaging images and recipient considerations. Campaign stages:
1. **Commercial selection**: products, price, stock, margin and demand.
2. **Experience readiness**: collection/PDP navigation, offer conditions, shipping/taxes/checkout and customer support.
3. **Proof/creative**: real product details, variant images, gift guides, demo, brand cues, accurate offer copy and creator licensing.
4. **Distribution**: Shopify owned store/email; paid Meta/Google Shopping/Search; organic creator/community/SEO as appropriate; no channel guaranteed.
5. **Operational readiness**: replenishment, staff, fulfillment, refunds, fraud, peak traffic/performance.
6. **Measurement**: actual net contribution, order/variant profitability, return/refund, new-vs-existing demand, customer quality and seasonality/holdouts.

## Attribution and promotion effects

A percent-off campaign often increases attributed conversion and AOV but may cannibalize full-price customers or drive low-margin items; evaluate **incremental realized profit after discount, media and returns**, not merely gross sales during the sale. Model and test price/threshold economics; avoid claiming an experiment is causal without credible control. Segment by margin and stock, not only customer LTV projections.
