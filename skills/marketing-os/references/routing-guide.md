# Routing guide — detailed request → workflow selection

Loaded on demand from [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). The router table there is sufficient for most requests; read this file when a request spans several specialists, the right depth is unclear, or you need the minimum route and references for a request type.

## Detailed routing rules

For requests to **launch a product, market or sell an offering, create a campaign, build a funnel, diagnose conversion or scale**, load the [marketing operating system](../WORKFLOWS.md) and route to only the relevant stage playbooks:

`commercial viability → buyer/occasion discovery → positioning/brand → offer/PDP → channel plan → creative/ad unit → launch QA → measurement/experiments → retention/scale`.

**When asked to research, understand, position, market, sell or launch a specific product/service/offering**, run [Offering Discovery → Marketing Plan (Workflow 1A)](../workflows/01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md). It gathers product/service truth, brand context, buyer jobs and buying situations, real alternatives, value/feature→benefit paths, drawbacks, objections, price/economics, assurances and claim proof, then delivers a [product/service dossier and prioritized marketing plan](../templates/offering-dossier-and-marketing-plan.md) with reusable bullets, descriptions, headlines, Meta ad units, creative directions, channel hypotheses and experiments. Use the [adaptive intake](../templates/offering-discovery-intake.md) and [offering research reference](offering-research-and-marketing-planning.md). Brand research is helpful, not a blocking prerequisite: use approved identity/voice when available, or a brief evidence-labeled scan and [Workflow 3A](../workflows/03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md) when needed.

**When asked about organic search, technical/on-page SEO, product/schema search visibility or search-driven sales**, use [SEO Workflow 5A](../workflows/05a-seo-and-organic-search.md) and [SEO fundamentals](seo-fundamentals-and-ecommerce.md). Diagnose intent, technical eligibility, page architecture, original content, merchant data, search appearance and search-to-purchase handoff.

**When asked about AI Overviews, Copilot/AI-answer citations, AEO/GEO or LLM visibility**, use [AEO Workflow 5B](../workflows/05b-aeo-ai-search-visibility.md) and [AI search reference](aeo-generative-search-and-citations.md). Build upon SEO and original expert evidence; measure available platform citation data without treating citation counts as visits, rankings or sales. Do not claim that llms.txt, arbitrary AI markup or mechanical chunking is required for Google AI Search.

**When asked to optimize a landing page, PDP, checkout, signup, lead form or conversion rate**, use [CRO Workflow 4A](../workflows/04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md), [CRO research](cro-research-and-experimentation.md) and [experiment brief](../templates/cro-experiment-brief.md). Diagnose traffic intent, offer, purchase friction, tracking and actual business outcomes before testing copy/layout.

**When asked how to promote a brand/product, distribute content, develop campaigns, use organic social or paid media across channels**, start with [Promotion Strategy 5C](../workflows/05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md) and [promotion research](promotion-fundamentals-and-integrated-distribution.md). Use paid/earned/shared/owned roles and customer, offer and brand evidence before choosing channels. Then select [Social Strategy 5D](../workflows/05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md) for hooks, calendars and community; [Creators/Collaborations 5E](../workflows/05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md) for micro-influencer, UGC, sponsorship and rights; or [PR/Earned Media 5F](../workflows/05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md) for genuine newsworthiness, releases, journalist pitches and newswire decisions. Do not equate syndicated paid copy with independent editorial coverage. Use [campaign brief](../templates/promotion-campaign-brief.md) and [outcomes review](../templates/promotion-outcomes-review.md).

**When asked for Google Ads, PPC, paid search, keyword research, match types, search creative, bidding, negative keywords or search-campaign audits**, run [Paid Search Workflow 5G](../workflows/05g-paid-search-google-ads.md), read [Google Ads fundamentals](paid-search-google-ads-fundamentals.md), and produce [campaign blueprint](../templates/google-ads-search-campaign-blueprint.md) plus [RSA asset sheet](../templates/google-search-rsa-and-assets.md) and [optimization/experiment plan](../templates/google-ads-optimization-and-experiments.md). Validate copy lengths with [RSA checker](../scripts/check_search_ads.py), but review factual claims, Google policies, primary conversion goals, 2026 AI Max URL/text settings, actual landing and spend approval separately. No launch without express authorization.

**When asked to diagnose, build a growth strategy for, merchandise or improve a Shopify/ecommerce store**, run [Shopify Store Diagnosis 1B](../workflows/01b-shopify-ecommerce-diagnosis.md) with the [store audit template](../templates/shopify-store-audit-and-roadmap.md), [Shopify strategy/economics](shopify-ecommerce-strategy-and-economics.md) and [curated ecommerce skills/resources](shopify-ecommerce-skill-and-resource-library.md). Choose only the specialists relevant to the observed bottleneck: [Catalog/Collection Merchandising 3B](../workflows/03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md), [Shopify CRO/Checkout 4B](../workflows/04b-shopify-ecommerce-cro.md), [Shopify Ecommerce SEO/Markets 5H](../workflows/05h-shopify-ecommerce-seo.md), [Shopify Shopping Ads/Feeds/Pixels 5I](../workflows/05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md), [Shopify Promotions/Bundles 5J](../workflows/05j-shopify-ecommerce-promotions.md), or [Shopify Lifecycle 9A](../workflows/09a-shopify-lifecycle-retention.md). Shopify store access is not presumed; distinguish publicly observed issues from admin-only metrics. Never assume unrestricted checkout edits or automatic consent to send campaigns.

**When asked for email marketing campaigns, newsletters, promotional messages, subject lines, preheaders, welcome/cart/checkout sequences or email copy**, run [Email Workflow 9B](../workflows/09b-email-marketing-campaigns-and-copy.md), read [Email Strategy](email-marketing-strategy-copy-and-creative.md), and use the [Email Creative Brief](../templates/email-campaign-and-creative-brief.md). For SPF/DKIM/DMARC, sender reputation, Gmail bulk rules, list health, unsubscribe and compliance, run [Deliverability 9E](../workflows/09e-email-deliverability-and-list-health.md) with the [Sender Audit](../templates/email-deliverability-and-consent-audit.md). Email legal requirements vary by jurisdiction; do not claim that US CAN-SPAM universally requires prior opt-in.

**When asked for SMS/text messaging, MMS, SMS copy, opted-in text subscribers, Postscript, Twilio, Attentive, Klaviyo SMS, A2P 10DLC or STOP/HELP**, run [SMS Marketing 9C](../workflows/09c-sms-text-and-messaging-marketing.md), [SMS Compliance Research](sms-text-marketing-and-compliance.md) and [SMS Campaign Brief](../templates/sms-campaign-and-compliance-brief.md). Enforce separate channel-specific permission, current provider/carrier rules, real message costs and quiet hours. Do not send without authorization.

**When asked for coordinated email/SMS flows, customer journey automation, segmentation and suppression**, run [Cross-channel Lifecycle 9D](../workflows/09d-cross-channel-lifecycle-and-segmentation.md), [Lifecycle Reference](lifecycle-orchestration-and-channel-governance.md) and [Journey Map](../templates/cross-channel-lifecycle-journey-map.md). Apply actual ESP/Shopify source and contact-state rules; protect against duplicate sends, customer purchase-after-queue, and mismatched consents.

**When asked for marketing analytics, dashboards, GA4/Shopify/Klaviyo analysis, measurement design, event schema or KPIs**, run [Marketing Analytics 8A](../workflows/08a-marketing-analytics-and-measurement.md), [Data Reference](marketing-analytics-data-model-and-reporting.md) and [KPI Dictionary](../templates/marketing-measurement-and-kpi-dictionary.md). For platform attribution, incremental impact, controlled holdouts, geo tests or MMM, run [Attribution/Experimentation 8B](../workflows/08b-attribution-incrementality-and-experiments.md) and its [Experiment Plan](../templates/marketing-attribution-and-incrementality-plan.md). For RFM, cohorts, CLV, retention or buyer segmentation, run [Customer Analytics 8C](../workflows/08c-customer-segmentation-rfm-and-clv.md) with the [RFM/Cohort Brief](../templates/customer-rfm-cohort-and-segment-plan.md). Never fabricate connected data, profit estimates, statistically significant effects, or customer attributes.

**When asked about data pipelines, ETL/ELT, marketing data engineering, GA4/BigQuery/GTM, Meta Pixel/CAPI/datasets/audiences, UTM tagging, Shopify/Etsy/Amazon API exports, tracking QA, offline conversions or reverse ETL**, run [Marketing Data Workflow 8D](../workflows/08d-marketing-data-pipelines-and-measurement.md). Select [ETL/modeling](marketing-data-engineering-and-etl.md), [GA4/Meta/UTM tracking](measurement-pixels-ga4-and-attribution.md), [Shopify/Etsy/Amazon integrations](commerce-data-shopify-etsy-amazon.md), [privacy-safe audiences/identity](audiences-identity-and-reverse-etl.md) and [curated agent skills/resources](marketing-data-tools-skills-and-resources.md) as relevant. Produce an [implementation blueprint](../templates/marketing-data-pipeline-blueprint.md), [event/UTM tracking plan](../templates/marketing-event-tracking-and-utm-plan.md), tests and reconciliation, and explicit authorization gates; never pretend to have access to live accounts.

**When asked to discover a brand, identify its voice or create a reusable brand copy library**, start with [Brand discovery → messaging (Workflow 3A)](../workflows/03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md). Investigate claimed vs expressed vs customer-perceived identity, goals, advantages, personality, perspective, proof and vocabulary; then produce a [brand discovery brief](../templates/brand-discovery-intake.md), [voice reference](brand-voice-and-copy-system.md), and [approved messaging/copy library](../templates/brand-messaging-library.md). If a brand guide already exists, assess and adapt it rather than inventing an identity. 

**Follow the evidence, not the diagram mechanically.** For existing underperforming ads, start at [measurement diagnosis](../workflows/08-measure-diagnose-experiment.md); for selecting among products, start at [commercial diagnosis](../workflows/01-commercial-diagnosis.md); for an in-depth investigation of a specific offering, start at [offering discovery](../workflows/01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md); for a scoped copywriting job, use [creative production](../workflows/06-creative-production.md) without forcing a business audit. Each stage must transform inputs into a named decision and output; record gaps, stop conditions and the next workflow. Use [the operating brief](../templates/operating-brief.md), [creative production brief](../templates/creative-production-brief.md) and [experiment decision log](../templates/experiment-decision-log.md) as relevant.

**Required decision packet for multistep work:** `finding → evidence/status → implication → recommended intervention → concrete deliverable → success measure → next decision`. Distinguish **what we know** from **what to test**. A recommendation may be: fix shipping, change product images, rework price/offer, build brand associations, test a new ad angle, repair tracking, contact opted-in customers, or choose not to advertise yet. Never fabricate inputs to make the workflow appear complete.

## Cross-workflow options

**Promotion and paid search option:** A verified brand/offering dossier can feed [integrated promotion 5C](../workflows/05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md), [social content 5D](../workflows/05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md), [creator partnerships 5E](../workflows/05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md), [PR 5F](../workflows/05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md) or [Google Ads paid search 5G](../workflows/05g-paid-search-google-ads.md) depending on buyer attention, intent, economic viability and available evidence. These routes are not compulsory for scoped copy-only tasks.

**Email, SMS and measurement option:** Email/CRM work requires actual lawful recipient eligibility and sender authority before creating or delivering external messages. Use [9B email](../workflows/09b-email-marketing-campaigns-and-copy.md), [9C SMS](../workflows/09c-sms-text-and-messaging-marketing.md), [9D orchestration](../workflows/09d-cross-channel-lifecycle-and-segmentation.md), [9E deliverability](../workflows/09e-email-deliverability-and-list-health.md), [8A analytics](../workflows/08a-marketing-analytics-and-measurement.md), [8B incrementality](../workflows/08b-attribution-incrementality-and-experiments.md) or [8C customer cohorts](../workflows/08c-customer-segmentation-rfm-and-clv.md) by question; examine the [research and skills index](email-sms-analytics-skills-and-resources.md). [Messaging plan checker](../scripts/check_messaging_plan.py) only catches structural gaps, not compliance or permission to send.

**2026 data integration update:** Existing [8D data pipelines](../workflows/08d-marketing-data-pipelines-and-measurement.md) coordinates three specialist implementation paths: [8E event/pixel instrumentation](../workflows/08e-event-instrumentation-and-conversion-qa.md), [8F marketplace extraction/reconciliation](../workflows/08f-marketplace-etl-and-reconciliation.md) and [8G identity/audiences/UTMs](../workflows/08g-audience-activation-and-utm-governance.md). Consult [verified 2026 API notes](2026-platform-data-api-updates.md) before coding. For concrete specs use [event destination/consent matrix](../templates/event-destination-and-consent-matrix.md), [marketplace extraction contract](../templates/marketplace-extraction-and-reconciliation-contract.md), [audience brief](../templates/audience-activation-and-suppression-brief.md) and the [offline pipeline manifest linter](../scripts/check_marketing_data_plan.py). All tests are structural until actual platform/account evidence is observed. Do not turn data availability into permission to share buyer identities.

**Ecommerce option:** Shopify stores need the same brand/offer/buyer positioning principles as any business, plus specific catalog, theme, Markets, Search & Discovery, pixels, checkout, inventory, merchant feeds and email/Flow mechanics. Start with [1B](../workflows/01b-shopify-ecommerce-diagnosis.md) for full-store requests; route narrowly for scoped tasks. Source current official Shopify docs and use the [ecommerce skill/resource index](shopify-ecommerce-skill-and-resource-library.md) for complementary Shopify developer skills—never pretend they are installed or authoritative business-performance evidence. See the [worked Shopify plan](../examples/shopify-ecommerce-end-to-end.md).

**Search and conversion option:** A single offering can need [SEO 5A](../workflows/05a-seo-and-organic-search.md) to be discovered, [AEO 5B](../workflows/05b-aeo-ai-search-visibility.md) to answer questions accurately in AI-mediated discovery, and [CRO 4A](../workflows/04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md) to turn relevant visits into informed, profitable outcomes. Use the shared [audit](../templates/search-and-conversion-audit.md) and [worked example](../examples/seo-aeo-cro-from-offering.md). Don't present these as separate hacks or impose all three on a simple ad-copy request.

**Grounding and live-data option:** Before strategy or copy, ground in real transcripts, reviews, saved research and (with access) Search Console/GA4 data rather than model priors. For multi-channel content, write one master asset and transform it per platform.
