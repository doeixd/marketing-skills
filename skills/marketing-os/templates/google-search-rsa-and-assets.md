## Combination and destination QA
- [ ] Every headline self-contained or safe if displayed in first/second/third positions.
- [ ] Descriptions stand alone; claims and qualifiers remain together.
- [ ] No unsupported absolute, false discount, invented shipping/returns, trademark confusion, misleading editorial style.
- [ ] At least 3 headlines and 2 descriptions, no more than 15/4. All lengths valid.
- [ ] Distinct *buyer* angles, not 15 keyword repeats; actual brand cue.
- [ ] Primary search phrase/category appears naturally where useful.
- [ ] Every visible claim matches real landing page product/price/policy.
- [ ] All assets, generated variations, AI Max URL expansion and rights reviewed.
- [ ] Live previews and current Google ad policy approval checked before publishing.
- [ ] [Check programmatically](../scripts/check_search_ads.py) with draft JSON contract; automation doesn't validate truth.
