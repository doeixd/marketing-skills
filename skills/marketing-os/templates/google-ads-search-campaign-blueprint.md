## B. Keyword and intent research
| Theme | Candidate keyword / term | Source and date | Buyer job and intent | Match broad / "phrase" / [exact] | Real landing URL | Estimated demand/CPC (source) | Negative/overlap risk | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Brand vs nonbrand decision:** separate if useful; note attribution/cannibalization risk.

**Competitor term decision:** legal, ad/landing relevance, brand fit and actual alternatives.

**Negative keyword matrix:**
| Term/variant | Negative match/scope | What unwanted search it blocks | Why genuinely irrelevant | Important wanted query it might block | Approval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Negative keyword semantics differ from positive match. Do not apply generic lists without checking actual business fit.

## G. Launch authorization and safeguards
- [ ] Real brand/offering facts and landing claims verified.
- [ ] Approved audience, geography, inventory/capacity and budgets.
- [ ] Measurement, correct primary goals and conversion values tested.
- [ ] Keywords, negatives, brand/AI Max expansions and URL lists approved.
- [ ] Search ads and all generated assets verified for policies and factual claims.
- [ ] Sitelinks, URLs, forms/checkout, mobile and tracking function.
- [ ] Start PAUSED; **explicit approval** required to enable or spend.
- [ ] Monitoring owner, frequency, spend anomaly alerts and rollback ready.
- [ ] Campaign created only if tool authorization and user permission exist.
