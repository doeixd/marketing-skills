# PR newsworthiness gate, press release, media kit and journalist pitch

Use [PR Workflow 5F](../workflows/05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md) and [PR/newswire reference](../references/pr-press-releases-newswires-and-earned-media.md). Do not fabricate contacts, quotations, news or awards.

## Newsworthiness first
| Candidate fact/event | Date/source | Who outside company cares and why | Timeliness | Impact | Local/trade/consumer relevance | Novelty/human interest | Verified? | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Gate:** independent/newsroom-relevant? niche relevant? owned update only? no story? If weak, prefer helpful blog, product announcement email or useful expert content over forced PR.

## Press release draft (factual, approved before publishing)
**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE** *(only when true / approved; otherwise state actual timing)*

**[Factual headline: verified news and consequence]**

*[Optional specific subhead]*

**[Verified dateline / actual date]** — [Lead paragraph: who, what, when, where, why it matters. Do not invent locations or timing.]

[Real context: evidence, credible details, buyer/public implications and reasonable limits.]

**[Actual spokesperson name/title] said:** “[Quote supplied and approved by them; never invent one.]”

[Additional verified details, method/FAQ, relevant product/service information and permitted link.]

**About [company]** — [Approved, accurate, short boilerplate.]

**Media contact:** [Name, role, verified publicly releasable contact details]

**Media assets:** [Authorized logos, photos, fact sheet, background/permissions]

**Fact/source IDs, review and approval:** [ ]

## Journalist-specific pitch draft (not auto-sent)
- **Outlet/beat and why relevant:** [exact recent example/date]
- **Email subject:** [news/information for that beat, not "huge opportunity"]
- **Opening line:** [their audience + real angle]
- **2–3 confirmed facts:** [source IDs, dates]
- **Useful materials:** [real expert/interview/research/photos available]
- **Close:** [one easy response question; no manufactured relationship]
- **Follow-up/decline policy:** [limited, respectful; honor stop]
- **Verified public pitch channel:** [do not invent private emails]
- **Owner approval to send:** pending
