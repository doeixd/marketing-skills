# Creator / influencer / co-marketing partnership brief

Use with [Creator Workflow 5E](../workflows/05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md) and [research and disclosure guide](../references/influencers-collaborations-and-community-partnerships.md). This is a planning/contract input, not a substitute for legal review.

## 1. Partnership purpose
- Brand and approved product/claims:
- Specific customer/job and buying occasion:
- Desired outcome: useful content, access to relevant audience, independent opinion, paid reach, referral, event or education:
- Model: gifted / paid endorsement / UGC production / paid Partnership Ad / Spark Ad / affiliate / co-marketing / independent earned mention:
- Genuine value for the creator's or partner's audience:
- Product availability, price, logistics and audience geography:

## 2. Candidate evaluation
| Creator/partner | Verified profile/url | Relevant audience evidence | Content quality + genuine fit | Engagement-quality observations | Brand safety | Estimated fee/effort | Red flags/unknowns | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Never invent creators, contacts, audience demographics, reach or response rates.

## 3. Creative collaboration
- Creative premise and audience need:
- Product truth, must-demonstrate elements, honest limitations:
- Creator latitude: personal experience, opinion and script direction:
- Deliverables (posts, Stories, videos, raw cutdowns, aspect ratios, captions, photos):
- Opening hook, demonstrations, useful body and appropriate next step:
- Product sample/training and support:
- Accessibility, caption/safe zones, brand cues:
- Review checkpoints, dates, revisions and deadline:
- **Never require a positive review or fabricated product experience.**

## 4. Compensation, disclosures, permissions
- Cash/gifts/perks/commission and exact terms:
- FTC or relevant local **material-connection disclosure**; format/on-video/on-image, caption and platform tool:
- Creator approval and platform features (Meta Partnership Ads/TikTok Spark Ads):
- Organic usage? Brand repost? Paid boosting/whitelisting? **Each separately:**
- License territory, duration, medium, edits, raw files, exclusivity, cancellation, revocation:
- Music, talent, footage, brand trademarks and third-party licenses:
- Product shipping and expenses; tax/invoicing as relevant:
- Review/approval and legal owner; contract signed?:
- Rights expiry calendar/check:
- Data privacy and audience metrics sharing permissions:
