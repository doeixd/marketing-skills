# Product and campaign intake

Fill what is known. Missing fields are **not** permission to invent details. Ask only questions indispensable to the current request; otherwise mark gaps and proceed conservatively.

## Product and evidence

- Business / brand name:
- Product name and SKU / variant:
- URL and destination:
- Product/category details and images:
- Verified features, materials, dimensions, care:
- Price and current offer, eligibility and dates:
- Stock / fulfillment / shipping / returns:
- Known limitations, drawbacks, exclusions:
- Source links / date verified:
- Authentic reviews, support queries, interviews / date and sample:
- Competing options and likely substitute products:

## Campaign

- Objective and conversion event:
- Customer situation / stage of awareness:
- Geography, language, and placements:
- Image/video assets, safe zones, creative constraints:
- Desired tone, banned words, proof standard:
- CTA and destination:
- Current campaign data and prior tested angles:
- Brand positioning and unique selling evidence:

## Deliverable

- Number of **distinct positioning concepts**:
- Preferred copy length and any hard limits:
- Include description / creative brief / landing-page recommendations?
- Output format: human-readable / JSON / CSV?
- Need a controlled experiment design or exploratory variants?

## If input is a bare URL

Inspect it and its linked product specs/offer/FAQ first; state what you could and could not verify. Do not force the user to manually supply information already plainly available.

- Differentiating product capabilities and evidence:
- Brand name, recognizable visual/verbal assets and rules for use:
- Known brand associations vs desired associations:
- Main brand-building, conversion or hybrid objective and time horizon:
- Availability, price, website checkout, shipping and trust obstacles that copy cannot resolve:
- Test hypothesis and outcome that would change our mind:
