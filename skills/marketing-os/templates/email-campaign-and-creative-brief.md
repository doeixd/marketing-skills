## Preflight
- [ ] Sender technical setup and applicable Gmail bulk rules verified.
- [ ] Contact marketing permission/eligibility, opt-out and suppression valid; no purchased/unconsented list assumed.
- [ ] Product/variant, claim, offer, price, shipping and landing verified.
- [ ] Personalization fallback, all links/UTMs, HTML/plain-text, accessibility and mobile rendering tested.
- [ ] No fake urgency/endorsement or invented postal address; real unsubscribe and one-click header if applicable.
- [ ] Flow collisions, purchase/refund/complaint suppression and schedule handled.
- [ ] Business outcome + delivery/unsubscribe/complaint guardrails and test plan.
- [ ] Human/legal/brand approval **and** explicit send permission: pending.
