## C. Hook development worksheet
| Asset | Buyer situation | Immediate visual (first frame/thumbnail) | First words (spoken/overlay/headline) | Promise/payoff in body | Specific proof | CTA | Claim IDs | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use multiple **hook mechanisms** (visual demonstration, legitimate contrast, buyer question, practical advice, genuine perspective, honest story), not mere sentence variations.

**Hook truth check:** Does the body deliver the promise? Is the brand/product attributable? Is the question genuinely relevant? Would a customer feel misled by the title? Are claims and creator experiences real?
