# Worked examples — product/service promotion across organic, partners, PR and paid

**Fictional scenarios; no claims about a real company or campaign.** Use [Promotion Workflow 5C](../workflows/05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md) and [campaign brief](../templates/promotion-campaign-brief.md).

## Scenario A: A vintage-inspired patterned ring

**Known in hypothetical:** Product listing calls it a vintage-inspired ring; genuine photo shows ornamental detail. **Unknown:** brand, material, price, sizes, origin, policies, reviews, budget, customer research.

### Buyer hypotheses and content

| Candidate buyer occasion | Potential motivation (hypothesis) | Content and hook | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-selected expressive jewelry | Enjoy ornate design | Actual close-up: "A closer look at this pattern" | Real high-resolution product visuals |
| Gift consideration | Recipient style may matter | "Choosing a ring for someone? Start with their style." | Actual size help and verified delivery/returns |
| Product comparison | Evaluate the design vs alternatives | "What details matter in a ring listing?" | Exact specs, true photos, actual alternatives |

No "hypoallergenic", "handcrafted", "heirloom", "fast shipping" or guaranteed perfect gift without proof. Request official brand identity before publication.

### PESO decision
- **Owned:** accurate PDP with real scale/specs, size/return policy and working checkout.
- **Shared:** Instagram carousel of actual details; short Reel where the opening frame shows the product, and comments answer real questions. No fake UGC.
- **Earned:** **no press release** merely because an ordinary SKU is for sale. An original collaboration/exhibition with a real demonstrable story could create niche press relevance *if it actually happens*.
- **Paid:** consider a small Meta test or paid stylist demonstration only after verified economics and landing readiness. A creator requires disclosed compensation, licensed usage and genuine experience; "micro" isn't automatically better.
- **Measurement:** qualified product visits, product revenue/contribution, actual returns, buyer questions and brand recall where measurable. Likes and raw views aren't a profitability claim.

## Scenario B: Hypothetical scoped AI workflow assessment service

**Illustrative concept only:** "Two-week workflow review" could include a kickoff, process inventory, assessment and findings document; verify actual delivery, price, staff, data/privacy terms, qualifications and outcome limitations.

**Potential buyers:** operational manager, business owner and possibly IT reviewer, with different objections (time, data security, generic advice, lack of ROI). Need real interviews or CRM signals.

**Source content first:** create a substantive how-to from verified team experience: *what to examine before automating a repeatable business process*. Demonstrate a genuine method and realistic constraints.

**Platform derivatives:** a LinkedIn post explaining three real steps with a clear example; a short video showing a permitted anonymized process; a useful email to consented subscribers; a live workshop with a real organization if both sides confirm.

**Collaboration:** a relevant industry association or credible educator could co-host a skills session if the audience gains genuine utility. Record mutual value, permissions, attendee privacy, deliverables, event budget and follow-up.

**PR gate:** a service announcement alone likely lacks external impact. If a **real** locally relevant study with transparent method/findings exists, create an approved factual press release and a beat-specific journalist pitch. No invented "new study" or spokesperson quote, and paid newswire syndication is not independent reporting.

**Paid trial:** search or LinkedIn may suit certain purchase-intent profiles after lead quality, close rate and service capacity are known. Use qualified pipeline/won revenue rather than volume of cheap forms.

**Key lesson:** The correct promotion plan can exclude a platform, influencer, press release or paid media entirely. Every channel must have a specific buyer-facing job, credible assets, permission and success definition.
