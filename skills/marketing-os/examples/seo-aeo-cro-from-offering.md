# Worked example: one offering across SEO, AI answer discovery, and conversion

**Fictional case:** a ring retailer has a real, detailed vintage-inspired ring product page, but we are *not given* actual Search Console, AI citation, analytics, price, materials or sales data. The point is to show a **correct reasoning path**, not imaginary performance figures.

## Stage A — Offering and buyer context

Read the [offering dossier](../templates/offering-dossier-and-marketing-plan.md) and [Brand Discovery](../workflows/03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md). Known facts in the hypothetical: ring, vintage-inspired design and visible pattern. Unknown: exact material, sizing, shipping, returns, demand by occasion and branded voice. Candidate jobs (not real buyer evidence): browsing for self-expression and buying as a gift.

## Stage B — SEO search map

| Potential query/situation | Appropriate page | Evidence needed | Commercial next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| "vintage style ring" | Product or applicable collection page | Actual product variants, descriptive photos, verified specs | See product and sizes |
| "how to find a ring size for a gift" | Original helpful guide if brand can give reliable sizing guidance | Practical method, caveats, actual sizing/exchange policy | Link to relevant styles |
| "ornate vs minimalist ring" | A comparison only if it gives genuinely useful examples | Honest style/fit tradeoffs and real photos | Explore style candidates |
| Branded product search | Accurate product page | Brand/SKU identifiers and catalog facts | Select variant and buy |

**Technical checks (not assumed findings):** Inspect robots/noindex, 200 status, canonical, internal links, responsive HTML, visible title/H1, page performance, and merchant Product/Offer markup only if the real page is eligible. Validate that price and availability match visible page and actual feed. No fictional star ratings or fabricated review markup.

**Draft example title, provisional:** "Vintage-Inspired Patterned Ring | [Approved Brand Name]". Replace bracketed text from official brand guide. Snippet text should describe actual product details, not keyword-stuff. Google's title/snippet systems may rewrite the displayed result.

## Stage C — AEO question-quality map

Question: "What is a good vintage-inspired ring for someone who prefers detailed designs?"

**Appropriate site contribution:** a page identifying the actual design, showing clear photos and context, realistic sizing and materials facts once verified, plus what type of buyer may dislike ornate detailing. Provide direct truthful information **for readers**.

**Bad AEO action:** create dozens of near-identical "best ring" FAQs, buy synthetic citations, add fake rating/review schema or claim `llms.txt` makes Google AI Overview recommend the item.

**Measurement:** use actual GSC and (where accessible) Bing AI Performance citation/grounding-query trends; do not claim citations from absent data. Citation doesn't necessarily equal visits or purchases.

## Stage D — CRO opportunity diagnosis

Hypothetical symptom supplied by business owner: "people click an ad but few orders." Do **not** infer that the ring is unattractive or the copy is weak.

Investigate competing explanations:
- Landing page failure (slow/incorrect URL); mismatch between advertised design and available variant.
- Price, materials, measurements, product photos, returns, shipping and checkout costs insufficiently clear.
- Buyers may have clicked for aesthetic interest but had no purchasing intent.
- Tracking, stock, delivery timing or account friction.

**Possible P0 fix:** If a size selector is actually broken, repair it and perform task-based mobile QA without testing which broken version users prefer.

**Possible P1:** If valid policies exist but aren't shown, surface them and see whether customers understand them. If key photos are absent, request authentic photos.

**P2 controlled experiment:** Only with enough volume, test a verified product-detail-first hero against an occasion-led hero; hold prices and eligibility stable, track product-level purchase rate and contribution, returns, SRM, latency and actual customer satisfaction. Never declare lift based on a screenshot or early one-day result.

## Integrated decision plan

1. Verify product attributes/brand voice before any externally published claims.
2. Fix search/index and checkout blockers first, with measurable QA.
3. Improve one high-intent product page with its real proof and buyer objections.
4. Produce genuinely helpful original informational content only if real buyers have that question.
5. Test distinct messages/visuals when evidence and traffic support testing.
6. Evaluate qualified organic visits, actual purchase contribution, return rate and (separately) AI citation trends.

**Takeaway:** SEO helps suitable buyers discover the offering; AEO strengthens factual answer resources and monitors limited citation surfaces; CRO helps those buyers evaluate and purchase confidently. The same **truthful brand/offering evidence** connects all three. Do not inflate any metric into a causal claim.
