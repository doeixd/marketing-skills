# Worked example: Shopify merchant → product insight → SEO/CRO/ads/retention plan

**Illustrative only.** A fictional Shopify jewelry store sells vintage-inspired patterned rings. We have one real-looking but hypothetical product photo and listing; **we do not have** admin access, Shopify plan, materials, actual price/inventory, market list, reviews, gross margin, Search Console or ad results. Do not reuse any hypothetical figure as a store fact.

## 1. Start with merchant diagnosis ([1B](../workflows/01b-shopify-ecommerce-diagnosis.md))

**Scope**: Shopify online store; one collection and its product listings; buyer situation could be expressive self-styling or gift consideration (hypotheses). Obtain real Shopify theme/plan/channel status and source-of-truth product export. No claim that a publicly visible page proves a feed error or poor CVR.

**Critical unknowns**: real variant materials/sizes, product photography/scale, Shopify Markets shipping, returns, policy, margin, current channel apps, consent and customer purchase reasons. First intervention is to request these inputs, not to publish new ads.

## 2. Merchandising and product truth ([3B](../workflows/03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md))

| Field | Example status | Marketing implication |
|---|---|---|
| Product type: vintage-inspired ring | Seller-stated hypothetical listing | Candidate product/category wording |
| Visible ornamental detail | Observed hypothetical photo | Product-photo closeup and concrete copy |
| Material/size/origin | Unknown | No nickel-free, handmade, durable or "heirloom" copy |
| Variant availability and costs | Unknown | No claim of shipping speed, stock or discounts |
| Gift/occasion appeal | Hypothesis | Potential segment/test, not established customer demand |

**Candidate benefit bullet**: "Vintage-inspired patterned design — take a closer look at the surface detail." **NOT** "Handcrafted luxury you'll treasure forever."

**Collection**: if several actual products exist, compare buyer-relevant attributes (style, color, verified material and size), sensible filters and stock; if only one ring exists, don't invent a collection or multiple options.

## 3. Shopify organic SEO ([5H](../workflows/05h-shopify-ecommerce-seo.md))

Potential search intent:
- "vintage inspired ring" → real PDP/collection based on assortment
- "patterned ring" → actual ring detail
- "ring sizing for gifts" → only a reliable actual sizing/returns guide
- "ornate vs simple ring" → only an honest useful comparison with real product options

Check actual `/products/` and `/collections/` pages, theme title/H1, crawlable Liquid links, product/variant canonical, real Product/Offer markup, live Shopify/Google feed price/stock and GSC indexing. **Do not assert Shopify auto-generated schema means rich-result approval.**

## 4. Ecommerce CRO ([4B](../workflows/04b-shopify-ecommerce-cro.md))

Ask first-time shoppers to locate the product and explain size, material, total price, delivery, returns and how to choose a variant. If any are missing, **fix information first**. If a real selector doesn't work, repair and regression-test it instead of A/B testing broken UX.

Possible test, **only with viable volume**: first product image closeup vs authentic on-body scale image. Primary outcome net contribution/order, secondary add-to-cart and suitability/returns; owner-approved, stable randomization, documented uncertainty. Shopify checkout editor scope must respect plan limits.

## 5. Shopify Google & Meta campaigns ([5I](../workflows/05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md))

Before spend:
- Google & YouTube/Merchant Center and Meta channel are genuinely installed/approved; actual variants/identifiers/catalog prices/markets match store.
- Shopify Web Pixels/purchase conversion events tested with privacy state and transaction ID/deduplication; platform ROAS not confused with profit.
- If Google Search intent exists, draft [RSA assets](../templates/google-search-rsa-and-assets.md) from real category/benefit and approve actual landing.
- Meta creative direction: opening product detail, brand cue, honest "look closer" angle; no invented customer testimony.
- Risk: a paid campaign could drive gift shoppers who can't find a real sizing or return policy. Fix first.

## 6. Shopify lifecycle ([9A](../workflows/09a-shopify-lifecycle-retention.md))

If valid consented segments exist:
- Welcome sequence with actual brand and product information;
- Abandoned checkout **only when real supported trigger/consent and not already handled by another app**;
- Postpurchase care instruction only from actual materials and product terms;
- Later gifting ideas for verified assortment and customer preference.

Jewelry is not always frequent replenishment. **Do not** schedule monthly winback discounts simply because a template suggests it.

## 7. Prioritized decision packet

| Priority | Candidate action | Why | Proof/approval needed | True outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Verify product/variant/policy facts and checkout functionality | Avoid misrepresentation and purchase failures | Shopify product data, actual settings and QA | Accurate buyer decisions |
| P1 | Improve PDP/collection findability and real media/fit | Reduce uncertainty, support SEO | Product photos, spec sheet, user tasks | Qualified purchases and return quality |
| P1 | Validate product sync, pixel values/consent | Avoid wasted ad optimization | Merchant Center/Meta and actual test orders | Reliable purchase values and eligibility |
| P2 | Test relevant search/Meta concepts with budget guardrails | Distinct buyer hypotheses | Customer demand + contribution + approval | Incremental net contribution if measurable |
| P2 | Deploy consented lifecycle only if useful | Educate/support and enable repeat gifting | Opt-in, suppressions, realistic occasion | Quality of retention and customer experience |

**Outcome:** a Shopify-specific marketing plan built from **brand/offering truth → catalog/SEO → CRO → campaigns → lifecycle and profit**, not disconnected optimization checklists. All proposed changes require merchant authorization.
