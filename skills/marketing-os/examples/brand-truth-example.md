## 7. Voice rules
- Sounds like: warm, precise, a little old-fashioned ("a ring with a past it never had", not "OMG obsessed").
- Vocabulary to use (from real customer language): "delicate", "detailed", "feels special", "gift".
- Words/claims never to use: "antique" or "vintage" without "-style" or "-inspired" (the rings are new); "handmade" until C4 is resolved; "luxury"; fake urgency.
- Formality, person, humour, emoji, regional spelling: second person; light humour; no emoji in ads; US spelling.
