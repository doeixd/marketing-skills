# Workflow 0 — Quickstart: the first 30 days with a product and a small budget

**Trigger:** "Where do I start?", "I have a product and $X", a first-time marketer, a new store or service with no marketing history, or a request that names no specific channel or problem.

**Purpose:** Get from nothing to one honest, measured marketing loop in 30 days, without running the full operating system. Every step points to the deeper workflow to use only if needed. Terms are defined in the [glossary](../references/marketing-glossary.md).

## 0. Ask five questions first

1. What do you sell, at what price, to whom, and where can you deliver it?
2. What does one sale earn you after product, shipping and fees (contribution margin)?
3. How much can you spend per month, and how many hours per week can you give this?
4. What exists already: website or listing, email list, social accounts, reviews, past customers?
5. What does success in 30 days look like (first sales, 20 leads, proof the offer works)?

Unknown answers are written `[NEEDS INPUT]`. Question 2 is blocking for any paid advertising.

## 2. The 30-day plan

| Week | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundations** | Create the [marketing context file](../templates/marketing-context.md). Fix anything that would lose a ready buyer: unclear price, shipping or returns, broken checkout or booking, no photos. Install and test conversion tracking. | Context file, a page or listing that can convert, one verified test conversion |
| **2. Buyers and message** | Read every review and support message; talk to 5–10 real or likely buyers ([Workflow 2](02-buyer-discovery.md)). Write one positioning sentence and three distinct message angles ([Workflow 6](06-creative-production.md)). | Buyer notes with verbatim phrases, 3 concepts |
| **3. One channel** | Choose **one** channel by where buyers already look and what the budget can support (table below). Launch with authorization and a spend cap ([Workflow 7](07-launch-qa.md)). | One live, tracked campaign or program |
| **4. Learn** | Compare results with real orders or leads, not platform numbers alone. Decide: continue, change the offer or page, change the message, or stop ([8H review](08h-marketing-review-cadence.md)). | One-page review and the next 30-day plan |

## 3. Match the channel to the budget

These are rough heuristics, not rules. The test is whether the budget can buy enough conversions to learn from: see the [media plan](05p-media-plan-and-budget-allocation.md) math.

| Monthly budget | Usually sensible | Usually premature |
|---|---|---|
| Time only | Reviews and referrals ([9F](09f-advocacy-reviews-referrals-and-reputation.md)), existing customers by email ([9B](09b-email-marketing-campaigns-and-copy.md)), organic social ([5D](05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md)), Google Business Profile, marketplace listing quality | Paid ads |
| Small (cannot buy ~50 conversions a month) | High-intent search on a few exact terms, marketplace ads on your best listing, boosting one proven organic post, local ads in a tight radius | Conversion-optimized social campaigns across several ad sets |
| Enough for ~50 conversions a week in one ad set | One conversion-optimized campaign on one platform, 3–5 creatives | Multiple platforms at once |

## 4. Rules for beginners

- One offer, one audience, one channel, one goal at a time. Add the second only when the first is measured.
- Fix the page or offer before buying more traffic to it.
- Never invent reviews, urgency, discounts or results to get started faster.
- Write down what you expected before you look at results, so you can tell learning from luck.

**Stop conditions:** no margin figure for paid ads; cannot legally sell or deliver in the target market; no way to observe a conversion. Fix these first.
