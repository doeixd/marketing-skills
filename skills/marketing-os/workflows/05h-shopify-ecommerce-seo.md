# Workflow 5H — Shopify organic search: technical eligibility, collections, products and Markets

**Trigger:** Shopify SEO audit, organic ecommerce growth, product/collection not indexed or underperforming, organic search snippets, Google merchant structured data, international search and catalog discoverability.

**Place:** Optional Stage 5 specialist, alongside [SEO 5A](05a-seo-and-organic-search.md), [AEO 5B](05b-aeo-ai-search-visibility.md) and [Offering 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md). Read [Shopify SEO reference](../references/shopify-ecommerce-seo-and-markets.md), [catalog guide](../references/shopify-catalog-merchandising-and-data.md) and [SEO/merchandising audit](../templates/shopify-search-merchandising-audit.md).

## 0. Search scope and data access

Identify domain, language/markets and actual Shopify product/collection URL patterns, theme/headless architecture, approved brand/product facts, GSC/analytics exports if available and goals (qualified organic discovery/commerce). Do not pretend a publicly accessible page reveals full GSC indexation or search impressions.

## 1. Map buyer questions and page intent

For each category/product: actual queries, customer terms, buying occasions, supported material/variant attributes, alternatives and offer questions. Prefer clear collection page for category browse; specific PDP for actual SKU; original guide for choice/care/fit; true policy/help for aftersales. Avoid low-value keyword pages or invented claims. Output `query/job → desired page → real evidence → gaps → conversion path`.

## 2. Inspect Shopify technical SEO

Audit real `robots.txt`, generated `sitemap.xml`, canonical tags, redirects/statuses, product and collection paths, variants/facets/sorting URLs and internal links/breadcrumbs. Shopify automates some defaults; real themes/apps may override them. Compare raw vs rendered HTML and ensure important product details/nav aren't JavaScript-only. For discontinuations, decide relevant redirect vs honest unavailable/404 page, not blanket homepage redirects.

## 3. On-page and merchant content

For priorities produce accurate title/H1/meta description, page introductions, feature→benefit bullets, verified specs, visuals/alt text, category copy, FAQ, real assortment links and appropriate CTA. Analyze actual Product/Offer JSON-LD and merchant eligibility vs current Shopify product variant price/currency/stock/identifiers/reviews/policies; check theme/app duplicate markup. Rich Results Test eligibility ≠ guaranteed appearance.

## 4. Markets and localization

For Shopify Markets check actual catalog/publishing per market, translations, local currency/pricing, service/shipping/returns eligibility, domain/subfolder strategy, hreflang, localized canonical, international sitemaps and accessibility of alternative locales. No invented country availability, misleading currency or copied machine-translated pages without review.

## 5. Prioritize and measure

P0 indexing/canonical/incorrect product data, P1 commercially meaningful product/collection relevance and navigation issues, P2 original content and UX experiments. Hand off tech changes via preview/QA, product facts through [Merchandising 3B](03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md) and [Ads 5I](05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md), buyer friction through [CRO 4B](04b-shopify-ecommerce-cro.md). Track query/page impressions, actual organic orders and net contribution, informed by brand and seasonality; changes aren't automatically causal.

**Deliver:** intent-to-page map, technical findings, Shopify URL/Markets checks, precise copy/metadata drafts, merchant-schema/feed proof and implementation roadmap. Do not publish without approval.
