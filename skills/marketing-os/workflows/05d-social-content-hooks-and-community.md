# Workflow 5D — Social strategy → hook concepts → content plan → community learning

**Trigger:** User wants an organic social strategy, calendar, hooks/captions, platform-native videos and carousels, founder content, community engagement, social SEO, cross-platform repurposing or explanation of weak social results.

**Place:** Optional sub-workflow of [Promotion 5C](05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md) / [Channel 5](05-channel-and-campaign.md). Use [social research and hook guide](../references/social-media-strategy-hooks-and-community.md), [content calendar](../templates/social-content-and-hooks-calendar.md), [brand voice](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), [product truth](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md), and [visual/video creative](06-creative-production.md).

## 0. Start with a job, not a platform trend

Identify current or desired audience, buyer situations, product/brand goals, approval voice, existing posts/channel data, visual assets, realistic team time, and exact CTA/destination. Decide whether the goal is **memory, information, social proof, dialogue, support, discovery, traffic, or sales**. A reply to a real customer may be more valuable than another promotional Reel.

## 1. Research people and communication norms

Inspect genuine content and conversations relevant to the target community, platform's official guide, actual creator/competitor posts *as creative examples only*, and first-party post insights. Record what customers ask and what formats they voluntarily engage with. Never infer sales from views, engagement, a viral screenshot or third-party advice. Avoid collecting private user data unnecessarily.

## 2. Choose platforms by channel role

Write an evidence-qualified matrix `platform | actual customer/job | content format | why fit | capacity | current performance | target metric`. For Instagram use real product visuals, feed/carousel, Reels/Stories for suitable buying journeys; TikTok vertical hook/body/close with genuine sounds/rights; YouTube utility/discovery and retention; LinkedIn original B2B expertise; Facebook groups/community rules; Pinterest visual/searchable product inspiration; forums conversation, not stealth promotion. Check account-specific tools and up-to-date native platform rules.

## 3. Build messaging pillars + creator's point of view

Choose 3–5 **meaningful** pillars, not 5 synonyms:
- Product/demonstration and real advantage.
- Help buyers choose, troubleshoot or compare fairly.
- Brand/person/credible process and behind-the-scenes.
- Trust, sourcing and real proof (with permission).
- Community, occasions, collaboration and customer questions.

Each pillar: `source fact | buyer relevance | series concept | voice rule | format(s) | CTA | metric`. Capture a **content idea bank**, including customer-originated questions, original research, owner experience and product footage. Don't invent brand founder stories or customer quotes.

## 4. Engineer honest hooks and complete stories

For each shortlisted idea choose:
1. **Audience/occasion**: who and when?
2. **Main value**: what information, entertainment, feeling or decision will the content deliver?
3. **First frame**: action/product/real demo/recognizable brand in mobile view.
4. **First words**: one concrete promise, question, comparison or observation.
5. **Body**: explain/show a credible reason with appropriately paced proof.
6. **Payoff**: satisfy hook, acknowledge honest limitation if material.
7. **CTA**: comment, save, explore, ask, attend, purchase, subscribe, as fits the role.

Explore 3–5 distinct hook families (demonstration, useful tip, real objection, honest surprise, opinion, occasion, story) *without* fake urgency/claims or bait-and-switch. Build complete caption/overlay/storyboard when requested; explicit visuals, rights and audio. Reject a hook if the body doesn't actually deliver it.

## 5. Produce a realistic editorial calendar

Template: [social content and hooks calendar](../templates/social-content-and-hooks-calendar.md). Record `publish window | platform | pillar | audience | hook | format | asset/source | script/caption | destination | partner/disclosure | owner | approval | KPI`. Prioritize consistency that the team can sustain; there is no universal daily quota or post time. One substantial source can make multiple **native adaptations**, not identical crossposts.

QA: accurate claims, actual visible product, representative images/rights, safe zones, meaningful closed captions, correct native specs, no private customer data, suitable hashtags/metadata and live checkout/page match. Publishing always requires explicit authorization.

## 6. Community operation + learning

Schedule realistic listening/reply moderation: useful responses, handling complaints, referral to support, clear escalation for legal/safety/crisis, respecting group rules and no impersonation/bought engagement. Track actual customer concerns back into [buyer discovery](02-buyer-discovery.md), [offering dossier](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md), [CRO](04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md).

Measure reach **and** retention/qualified comments, saves with context, brand/search interest, meaningful visits/leads/orders, long-term familiarity when measurable. Compare platform-matched posts and definitions; don't claim one post proves a reusable algorithm.

**Output:** channel-role recommendation, pillar map, hook bank + full first content batch, calendar with assets/captions/CTA/owners, response playbook, learning/experimentation plan. Refer to [promotion results template](../templates/promotion-outcomes-review.md).
