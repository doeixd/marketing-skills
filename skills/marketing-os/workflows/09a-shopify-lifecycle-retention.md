# Workflow 9A — Shopify lifecycle: email, SMS, customer segments, Flow and profitable repeat

**Trigger:** Shopify welcome/onboarding, abandonment recovery, postpurchase education, review requests, winback, SMS/email, customer loyalty/referrals, segments, recurring purchase, customer LTV or marketing automation.

**Place:** Stage 9 specialist. Read [Lifecycle reference](../references/shopify-lifecycle-retention-and-automation.md), [Retention 9](09-retention-and-scale.md), [Brand 3A](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), [Offering 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) and [Lifecycle Plan](../templates/shopify-lifecycle-and-retention-plan.md).

## 0. Establish real customer lifecycle and permissions

Identify natural repeat-purchase pattern, product usage/gift cycles, first/returning cohorts, margin, refund/complaint data, consent by email/SMS/WhatsApp, local laws, suppression and installed Shopify Messaging/Forms/Flow/third-party ESP. No assertion that a vendor app is installed just because store uses Shopify. Distinguish transactional from marketing consent and message types.

## 1. Map customer moments

`first discovery → email opt-in → browse/cart/checkout → paid purchase → fulfillment → usage/satisfaction → related occasion or product → recommendation or return`. Identify actual trigger, buyer concern, helpful message and relevant exit condition. Abandoned browse, cart and checkout are not interchangeable events. Shop app/POS restrictions and different consent rules may apply.

## 2. Design relevant segments and automations

Use real Shopify customer segments and authorized purchase history. Prioritize welcome/education, genuine abandonment assistance, postpurchase care, credible review request, relevant cross-sell/replenishment, thoughtful winback and referral only when relevant. For every flow define trigger, delay, recipient eligibility, exclusions (purchased/refunded/support issue), content/offer, actual product availability, sending channel, suppression and owner. Check double-send across Shopify Messaging and Klaviyo/etc.

## 3. Produce copy and operational plan

From brand and offering evidence draft subject, preheader, heading, concise benefit/answer, true product link/offer, real policy reassurance and CTA. Use segmentation by real interests and needs, not speculative personal attributes. No fake limited stock/urgency or reward conditioned on positive review. Shopify Flow for custom triggers and third-party integration where verified; Shopify Messaging and Autopilot require eligibility and **user approval** to activate sends.

## 4. Business and experience measurement

Measure consented recipients/delivery/open/click with caveats (e.g., email privacy), complaints/unsubscribes, qualified postpurchase behavior, repurchase and **incremental contribution**. Avoid treating "recovered revenue" as automatically caused by an email; some customers would have purchased anyway. Monitor refunds, discount cost, frequency fatigue and customer service. For naturally low-repeat items, favor seasonal gift occasions, care and referrals over false cadence.

## 5. QA / approval

Use [Lifecycle Plan](../templates/shopify-lifecycle-and-retention-plan.md): inventory links, actual eligibility, consent/suppression, automation overlap, exit conditions, timezone, message limits, correct checkout link, QA inbox/tests, data access, ROI guardrails and explicit approval. No activation or customer communication without permission.

**Deliver:** consented segment strategy, lifecycle/event map, prioritized flows, customer-safe copy, operational handoffs and testing/measurement plan.
