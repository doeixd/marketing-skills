# Workflow 4 — Offer design, merchandising and purchase-path readiness

**Trigger:** Positioning is complete, but buying requires price, stock, page, checkout, or policy readiness; high traffic with weak sales; returns or gift friction.

**Product/service-level context:** [Offering Discovery 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) assembles the exact delivery scope, price, limits, buyer objections, potential alternatives and claim register. Use its [offering dossier](../templates/offering-dossier-and-marketing-plan.md) to decide whether the necessary action is better copy, PDP evidence, an honest policy clarification, pricing changes or a real product/service improvement.

**When traffic is present but qualified conversion is weak**, run [CRO Workflow 4A](04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md) with the [CRO reference](../references/cro-research-and-experimentation.md) and [experiment brief](../templates/cro-experiment-brief.md). Diagnose actual visitor friction and economic outcomes before rewriting a hero headline or increasing spend.

## A. Distinguish the product from the offer

- **Product:** actual physical/digital attributes and outcomes.
- **Offer:** what buyer receives, net price, options, shipping, terms, extras and purchase conditions.
- **Experience:** proof, discoverability, mobile usability, support, checkout and after-sale treatment.

A better ad cannot repair an obviously inferior or misrepresented offer. Don't invent discounts, bonuses, low-stock urgency, reviews, return terms or free shipping.

## B. Answer buyer questions at the point of purchase

| Buyer question | Page or business action | Evidence / data |
|---|---|---|
| What exactly am I getting? | Images, variant selection, specifications, scale/fit | Product truth, photo set |
| Why is it worth this price? | Show relevant benefits and real differentiators; possibly alter offer | Buyer criteria, pricing/margin |
| Will this work or fit for me? | Dimensions, sizing, compatibility, demonstrations | Specs, real questions |
| Can I trust the seller? | Clearly identify business; authentic customer reviews, transparent contact/policies | Verifiable reviews and policies |
| When will it arrive? | Accurate fulfillment estimates and order deadlines | Operational estimates |
| What happens if it disappoints? | Actual return/exchange/warranty details | Current written policies |
| Can I buy quickly? | Mobile-friendly checkout, visible CTA, working payment and stock | Usability testing, checkout traces |

## C. Economics-aware offer options

Explore appropriate bundles, complementary products, product comparison/selection, volume thresholds, gifting options, transparent shipping, differentiated merchandising, subscriptions **only if product behavior justifies them**. Evaluate net contribution and effect on returns, not revenue alone. Never advise discounting solely because CTR is low.

## D. End-to-end message match

For each proposed ad, verify: **buying occasion → promise → image/video → click destination → hero product → variant → net price and terms → checkout**. If the ad mentions gifting, the page must answer gift objections. If the ad foregrounds material quality, specifications must substantiate it. If Meta automatically rearranges text combinations, every permissible variant must still be truthful.

## E. Decision gate

- **Block paid launch:** deceptive/unverified claims, broken checkout, stock unavailability, major fulfillment issues or known negative economics without an explicitly approved learning budget.
- **Improve page first:** unresolved high-impact objections or material uncertainty about price, fit, delivery or returns.
- **Ready:** accurate page, real product evidence, appropriate offer, working checkout, documented economics and useful conversion measurement.

**Handoff:** verified offer, PDP URL, page limitations and permitted claims → [channel strategy](05-channel-and-campaign.md), [creative production](06-creative-production.md).
