# Workflow 1B — Shopify store and ecommerce marketing diagnosis

**Trigger:** "Audit my Shopify store", "plan ecommerce growth", weak sales/contribution, uncertain merchandising, new store launch, scaling channels, or cross-functional conversion/marketing problems.

**Place:** Stage 1 specialist orchestration. Read [strategy/economics](../references/shopify-ecommerce-strategy-and-economics.md), [Shopify technical/analytics](../references/shopify-theme-performance-and-measurement.md), [skills/resources index](../references/shopify-ecommerce-skill-and-resource-library.md) and [audit/roadmap](../templates/shopify-store-audit-and-roadmap.md). Invoke [Offering 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md), [Brand 3A](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), [Merchandising 3B](03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md), [CRO 4B](04b-shopify-ecommerce-cro.md), [SEO 5H](05h-shopify-ecommerce-seo.md), [Ads 5I](05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md) and [Lifecycle 9A](09a-shopify-lifecycle-retention.md) as evidence warrants.

## 0. Establish scope and permission

Identify shop domain, store plan (especially Plus), active theme vs headless/Hydrogen, market/language, core categories/SKUs, brand style, business goal and constraints, apps, paid channels, whether admin reports/exports or source code are available. **Public URLs are not admin access.** Do not assume privileged store data. Need only ask the missing fact that blocks a consequential decision.

## 1. Reconcile commerce economics and data

Gather actual orders/revenue/net sales, discount, COGS, fulfillment/payment fees, shipping costs, returns, stock, new/returning mix, session trend and channel costs where authorized. Distinguish **orders and contribution** from mere Shopify reported conversion rate or ad ROAS. Flag [September 21–23, 2026 Shopify session definition update](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/discrepancies/session-measurement-update) and marketing report consolidation before comparing pre/post dates. If metrics are absent, give formula/measurement requests—not fictitious diagnoses.

## 2. Map store and buyer journeys

Examine brand and category recognition, nav, collections/filtering/search, actual PDP details/variants/media, cart, checkout/policies, mobile, real fulfillment promises, postpurchase and contact/support. Audit buyer tasks: first-time discovery, gift selection, variant/sizing, comparison, shipping/returns and repeat occasion. Record specific URL/observation, impact and uncertainty. Services/subscriptions require scope, access, delivery and recurring terms rather than generic catalog assumptions.

## 3. Shopify configuration and integrity

Check plan/theme/Markets, products and collections, metafields/metaobjects, Search & Discovery availability, variants and stock, sitemap/canonical/hreflang, feed integrations, theme apps and performance, checkout extension plan eligibility, Web Pixel events/privacy and consent. Do not assert that missing snippets in publicly visible HTML prove a connected catalog/CRM is broken without confirming settings.

## 4. Locate the binding business constraint

Classify likely primary issue:
- product/market/value mismatch;
- assortment/stock/price margin issue;
- discovery/organic search/ads channel problem;
- inadequate product proof or category organization;
- high-intent UX/checkout friction or false costs/policies;
- tracking/data corruption;
- delivery/returns problems;
- poor qualified retention or unsuitable lifecycle.
For each list alternative explanations and required confirming evidence. A higher CTR is not the business goal.

## 5. Prioritize an executable roadmap

Create a P0 **safety/measurement/broken purchase** queue, P1 **high-confidence buyer/commercial friction**, and P2 **testable strategic growth hypotheses**. Each: `URL/process → finding/evidence → buyer/business impact → fix/experiment → Shopify surface/app/plan check → owner/effort → primary outcome/guardrails → approval/rollback`.

Generate [store audit and roadmap](../templates/shopify-store-audit-and-roadmap.md) plus targeted briefs—not an undifferentiated 150-item checklist. Choose 1–3 next specialist workflows and explain why.

**Gate:** no live theme, catalog, pixel, checkout, automation, price, marketplace feed, ads or customer communication change without explicit permission and appropriate access. Source-based findings, speculative hypotheses and proposed changes stay separate.
