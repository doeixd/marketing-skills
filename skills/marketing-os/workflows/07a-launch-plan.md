# Workflow 7A — Launch plan: tier → readiness → sequenced timeline → launch day → post-launch

**Trigger:** Launching a product, feature, collection, store, service or major campaign; "launch plan", "go-to-market timeline", "launch checklist", a fixed launch date, or a relaunch.

**Place:** Coordinates the other stages around a date. [Workflow 7](07-launch-qa.md) is the pre-flight check for individual ads; this plans the whole launch. Inputs: offering dossier ([1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md)), positioning ([3](03-positioning-and-brand.md)), [media plan](05p-media-plan-and-budget-allocation.md). Output: a [launch plan](../templates/launch-plan.md).

## 1. Size the launch

| Tier | What it is | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New business, flagship product, or a change to positioning | Full plan, all relevant channels, weeks of preparation |
| 2 | Meaningful new product, feature or collection | Core channels: owned, email, selected paid and partners |
| 3 | Small addition or improvement | Announcement to existing customers; update pages |

Most launches are tier 2 or 3. Over-launching small news wastes attention and credibility.

## 2. Define success and the audience order

One primary goal and number (sales, signups, qualified leads, waitlist conversions), guardrails (margin, stock-outs, support load, refund rate), and the date results will be judged. Decide who hears first: existing customers and subscribers, waitlist, partners and creators, press, then cold audiences.

## 3. Check readiness (go/no-go gates)

| Area | Ready when |
|---|---|
| Product and stock | Sellable, deliverable, enough inventory or capacity for the high forecast; fulfilment tested |
| Offer and price | Final price, terms, guarantees and any launch offer approved; honest scarcity only |
| Message | Positioning, claim register and approved copy in the [context file](../templates/marketing-context.md) |
| Destination | Page or listing live on a hidden URL, tested on phones, checkout or booking verified with a test order |
| Tracking | Conversion events verified end to end; UTMs defined; dashboard ready |
| Assets | Creative for each channel in final formats; rights and disclosures cleared |
| People | Support briefed with FAQs; sales or partners enabled; owners named for each channel |
| Legal and platform | Required disclosures, regulated claims, promotion terms, ad-account status |

Any failed gate moves the date or reduces the scope. A late launch costs less than a broken one.

## 4. Sequence the timeline

Adapt the durations; small launches compress to days.

| Phase | Typical timing | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | T-6 to T-4 weeks | Positioning locked, assets briefed, page drafted, tracking planned, partner and press list built |
| Pre-launch | T-4 to T-1 weeks | Waitlist or early-access capture; teasers that are true; seed product to creators and reviewers with disclosure terms ([5E](05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md)); press under embargo only if genuinely newsworthy ([5F](05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md)); email warm-up; ad drafts built and reviewed |
| Final checks | T-1 week | Go/no-go review; test order; support dry run; schedule content; set account spend caps |
| Launch day | T | Run-of-show with times and owners: page live → customers and waitlist email/SMS → social and partners → press embargo lifts → paid ads on. Monitor orders, site, tracking, comments and support hourly |
| Sustain | T+1 to T+14 days | Proof from early buyers (with permission), objection-answering content, retargeting, second email to non-openers, partner posts |
| Review | T+7 and T+30 days | Results vs goal and forecast, what to keep running, retrospective |

## 5. Prepare for things going wrong

Write down, before launch: who can pause ads and scheduled posts; what happens on a stock-out, site outage, payment failure, pricing error or a damaging comment or review; a holding statement; and the rollback steps. See reputation handling in [9F](09f-advocacy-reviews-referrals-and-reputation.md).

## 6. Review and hand off

At T+7 and T+30 compare with the forecast using store or CRM truth, record what was learned about buyers, message and channels in the [decision log](../templates/experiment-decision-log.md), update the context file, and move the ongoing activity into the regular [review cadence](08h-marketing-review-cadence.md).

**Stop conditions:** any go/no-go gate failing at the final check; launch offer or scarcity claim that isn't true; no named owner for launch-day monitoring.
