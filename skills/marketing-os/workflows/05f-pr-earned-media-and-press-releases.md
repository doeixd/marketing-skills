# Workflow 5F — PR: newsworthiness → press kit/release → targeted media relations

**Trigger:** Launch announcement, milestone, product introduction, event, original research, press outreach, PR campaign, newsroom, press release, media pitch, newswire/distribution comparison or reputation/earned-media strategy.

**Place:** Optional [Promotion 5C](05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md) sub-workflow. Read [PR research](../references/pr-press-releases-newswires-and-earned-media.md), [press/pitch kit template](../templates/pr-newsroom-and-pitch.md), [brand context](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), [offering facts](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md), [promotion measurement](../references/promotion-fundamentals-and-integrated-distribution.md).

## 0. Decide what is actual news

Answer:
- What exactly happened, when, who is affected, and why should *someone outside the company* care?
- Which facts are verified with dates/data/method and who can speak to them?
- Is this local/community, trade, consumer, industry or general news?
- Is a real spokesperson available and authorized? Any legal/embargo/confidentiality constraints?
- Do readers need it now? Is claimed "first"/"only"/"breakthrough" verifiable?
- What independent reporting angle could a journalist pursue?

Apply timeliness, impact, proximity, novelty, prominence and human relevance. Score **strong news / niche relevant / owned announcement only / no story**. A new generic SKU need not be pitched as news.

## 1. Choose public-information strategy

| Strategy | Trigger | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Owned newsroom/company update | Useful announcement without clear third-party news value | Accurate web post/FAQ/press room |
| Tailored trade/local press pitch | Real expert/impact for a particular beat | Journalist-specific email + fact kit |
| Press release for significant event | Verifiable news, clear public who/what/when/where/why | Factual release with real authorized quote |
| Newswire distribution | Broad coverage/stakeholder/legal distribution needs and justified cost | Vendor comparison + approved release |
| Independent expert commentary | Team has verifiable knowledge related to an active story | Factual expert offer; no forced product mention |
| Paid native/sponsored story | Willing to buy labeled editorial-style exposure | Correctly disclosed paid content, **not earned media** |

A wire's paid distribution and syndication are NOT guaranteed independent coverage, qualified audiences, ranking or backlinks; compare pricing/scope in current vendor quotes rather than assume a cost.

## 2. Research media fit

Build a lean factual contact list from verifiable current public professional sources (not guessed emails): `outlet/beat | relevant published story/date | readership | verifiable pitch channel | legitimate angle | asset need | status`. Tailor to editorial audience, not only company message. Don't spam or promise an interview that isn't approved.

The 2026 Cision journalist survey emphasizes original data, verified experts, relevance and accuracy. **A polished mass AI pitch is not a shortcut.**

## 3. Assemble release, pitch and verification kit

Use [PR newsroom template](../templates/pr-newsroom-and-pitch.md):
- **Release**: clear factual headline, dateline, key news lede (5 Ws), real context, qualified numbers, authorized actual quote(s), boilerplate, working newsroom/media contact and licensed media.
- **Pitch**: short human subject + specific relevance to that journalist's beat/readers + 2–3 verifiable facts + actual interview/demo access/links + reply route. No mass-produced phony personalization.
- **Fact sheet**: who/what/how/when, specifications, impact methodology, limitations, corrections route, actual asset licensing.
- **Paid-wire decision**: newsworthiness, verified coverage/geography, cost, media targets, sponsor/paid distinction, measurement and review process.
- **Owned/social derivative**: contextual public announcement and useful buyer destination; do not reuse hyperbolic ad copy as release text.

Avoid inventing names, emails, quotes, press dates, partnerships, patent claims, numbers, testimonials, awards, records and reader circulation.

## 4. Legal, ethics, process gates

Review confidential info, third-party rights, permissions, endorsements, regulated claims, financial disclosure, embargo and brand approval. Mark sponsorship transparently; never disguise an advertorial or a paid wire syndication as independently earned endorsement. No sending, payments or publication without explicit sign-off.

Press contacts should not be automatically added to broad email marketing; honor applicable communications/privacy rules and requests to stop outreach.

## 5. Publication and follow-up

With user authorization: publish owned newsroom release, share embargoed kit only after agreed terms, send tailored limited pitches, coordinate spokesperson availability, distribute via wire only if justified/approved, answer questions promptly, monitor errors or corrections and publish genuine social derivatives. Respect independent editorial decisions and declined pitches.

## 6. Measure credibility and downstream outcomes

Distinguish:
- **Output:** releases distributed, *syndicated* copies, independently reported articles, credible mentions, reporter relationships.
- **Out-take:** accurate reader understanding and qualified discussion; credible brand recall when measurable.
- **Outcome/impact:** sourced qualified visits, relevant inquiries, reputation/trust movement, partner interest and business outcomes *with uncertainty*.

No "advertising value equivalency" as causal ROI; avoid inflated potential audience totals. If coverage fails, examine newsworthiness/beat relevance instead of just paying a larger wire.
