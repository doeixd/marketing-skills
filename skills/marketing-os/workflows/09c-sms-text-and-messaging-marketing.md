# Workflow 9C — SMS/text marketing: consent → useful occasion → concise copy → safe delivery

**Trigger:** Marketing texts, SMS/MMS, short code/10DLC, Postscript/Attentive/Twilio/Klaviyo SMS, Shopify Messaging SMS, WhatsApp campaigns, cart recovery texts, opt-in forms, STOP/HELP handling, compliance or SMS optimization.

**Place:** Stage 9 specialist. Read [SMS Strategy/Compliance](../references/sms-text-marketing-and-compliance.md), [SMS Brief](../templates/sms-campaign-and-compliance-brief.md), [Cross-channel 9D](09d-cross-channel-lifecycle-and-segmentation.md) and [Analytics 8A](08a-marketing-analytics-and-measurement.md). Refer to [Brand 3A](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md) and [Offering 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) for correct message and buyer relevance.

## 0. Determine whether SMS is appropriate

Identify time-sensitive/helpful buyer need, actual phone marketing consent and source, sending platform, sender registration/route, country/state and recipient time zone, current frequency and other email/SMS contacts, actual offer/product availability, conversion goal and send costs. If no meaningful urgency/value, recommend email, onsite account, support or other less disruptive method.

## 1. Privacy/legal and provider gate

Verify separate SMS opt-in (email opt-in does not confer SMS marketing rights), retention of consent proof, TCPA/state/CTIA/carrier requirements, jurisdiction-specific quiet hours, appropriate A2P 10DLC/toll-free/short-code/WhatsApp configuration where used, STOP/HELP and other revocation methods, and customer suppression across apps. Do not offer blanket legal certification; current counsel/provider rules control.

US FCC rules changed in Oct 2026 with some changes effective Nov 25, 2026; check current dates rather than applying announced rules prematurely. If consent uncertain or user requests sending to an unpermissioned list, block marketing send and propose lawful collection instead.

## 2. Define customer situation and content

Choose a single real action: check a genuinely available restock, attend an actual event, finish a valid eligible checkout, confirm an authorized appointment, see product selection, or learn product/care advice. Ensure message doesn't falsely imply surveillance, deadline, scarcity, personal relationship or formal order.

Draft 3–5 **distinct** messages: brand → specific customer-relevant value → truthful terms and product → single trustworthy URL/CTA → required STOP/help. Preserve brand tone and clear meaning without forced slang. Check emoji/Unicode, SMS segment cost (GSM-7/UCS-2), link trust and real destination. *A 160-character target is not a universal platform constraint; encoding and segmented texts change billable units.*

## 3. Cross-channel logic

Use [Journey 9D](09d-cross-channel-lifecycle-and-segmentation.md) to avoid simultaneous default email+SMS sends, set frequency budgets, SMS-only eligibility, local quiet hours, purchase/return/support exit and consent updates. Verify that Shopify/Klaviyo/Postscript/Twilio states match rather than assuming STOP propagates to all.

## 4. QA and measure

Test with approved internal numbers, real opt-out behavior, links, dynamic fallbacks, locale/timing, pricing, subscription details and send charges. Measure accepted/delivered vs actual click/qualified purchase, STOP rate, complaints and **net incremental contribution**, not reported "near 100% open rate." Where feasible run holdout among legally eligible recipients; record all costs including message segments and discounts.

**Deliver:** SMS suitability decision, consent/provider QA, campaign or flow matrix, message variants and send timing, suppression and quiet-hour rules, financial model and experiment/approval plan. **No live sending, registration, contact uploads or spending without authorization.**
