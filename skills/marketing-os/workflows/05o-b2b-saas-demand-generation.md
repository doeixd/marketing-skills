# Workflow 5O — B2B and SaaS demand generation: ICP → accounts → demand → qualification → handoff → activation

**Trigger:** B2B or SaaS marketing; ideal customer profile, account-based marketing, lead generation, LinkedIn or outbound programs, MQL/SQL definitions, lead scoring, sales handoff, pipeline targets, free trial or freemium conversion, product-led growth, webinars or events for leads.

**Place:** Optional specialist in [Stage 5](05-channel-and-campaign.md). It reuses buyer research ([2](02-buyer-discovery.md)), positioning ([3](03-positioning-and-brand.md)), landing pages ([4D](04d-landing-page-build.md)), LinkedIn/search ads ([5L](05l-paid-social-and-video-platforms.md), [5G](05g-paid-search-google-ads.md)) and email ([9B](09b-email-marketing-campaigns-and-copy.md)); retention is [9G](09g-subscription-retention-and-churn.md).

## 1. Define the ICP from evidence

Analyze closed-won, closed-lost and churned accounts: industry, size, tech stack, trigger events, use case, deal size, sales cycle, retention. The ICP is the segment that buys fastest **and** stays. Map the **buying committee** (user, champion, economic buyer, technical/security/procurement reviewers), what each needs to believe, and who can veto. With no customer history, label the ICP `hypothesis` and plan interviews.

**Deliverable:** ICP statement, disqualifiers, committee map, trigger events.

## 2. Choose the motion

| Motion | Fits | Marketing's main job |
|---|---|---|
| Product-led (self-serve trial or freemium) | Low price, fast time-to-value, individual can adopt | Qualified signups, activation, trial-to-paid |
| Sales-assisted | Mid-market deals needing a demo or evaluation | Qualified pipeline, enablement content |
| Account-based (named accounts) | High contract value, few possible buyers | Reach and engage the committee at chosen accounts |

For account-based work, tier the list: 1:1 (bespoke research and outreach), 1:few (clusters sharing a problem), 1:many (programmatic). Select accounts by fit **and** evidence of timing; third-party "intent" data is a weak, noisy signal on its own.

## 3. Create and capture demand

- **Capture** existing demand first: high-intent search terms, comparison and alternatives pages, review sites, pricing page clarity.
- **Create** demand where the committee pays attention: useful original content and data, practitioner communities, LinkedIn (organic from real people plus paid to named accounts), webinars and events with a clear follow-up, partners and integrations, customer stories published with written permission.
- **Outbound email:** relevant, researched, one clear ask, working opt-out. Rules differ by country: US CAN-SPAM permits unsolicited B2B email with identification and opt-out; the EU/UK and Canada are stricter and often need consent or a narrow exemption. Volume sending must meet [bulk-sender requirements](../references/email-deliverability-consent-and-reputation.md); don't burn the main domain's reputation or use purchased lists.
- Don't gate everything. Gate only what a buyer would fairly trade details for; ungated content often produces more qualified demand.

## 4. Define stages and the handoff in writing

Agree with sales on one definition each for lead, **MQL** (fits ICP and showed intent), **SQL** (sales accepted, real opportunity criteria), **PQL** (product usage showing value reached), opportunity and closed-won; a response-time commitment; and what happens to rejected leads. **Score** on fit (firmographic) and intent (behavior) separately, then validate the score against actual conversion to opportunity each quarter. A score that doesn't predict outcomes should be dropped.

Send qualified-stage events back to ad platforms as offline conversions so campaigns optimize for qualified pipeline, not form fills.

## 5. Activate trials and new accounts (PLG and onboarding)

Define the **activation event** (the first moment of real value) from retention data. Then: reduce steps to reach it; onboarding emails and in-app guidance triggered by what the user has or hasn't done; human help for high-fit accounts; a trial-end sequence that states what was achieved and what is lost, without false urgency. Track signup → activated → paid by cohort and source.

## 6. Measure the business, with long cycles in mind

Pipeline created and its source, stage conversion rates, win rate, sales cycle, CAC payback, LTV:CAC, net revenue retention. With long cycles and many touches, click attribution misses a great deal: add a required "how did you hear about us?" field, CRM campaign influence, and cohort views of accounts reached vs not reached. Review in [8H](08h-marketing-review-cadence.md).

**Stop conditions:** no agreed ICP or stage definitions (fix before spending on leads); sales cannot follow up within the agreed time; churn so high that acquiring more customers loses money.
