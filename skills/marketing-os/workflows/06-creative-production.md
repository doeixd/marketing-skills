# Workflow 6 — Customer insight → creative concept → Meta ad unit

**Trigger:** A campaign job and product/offer are selected; the task is to generate ad concepts, copy, visual guidance and a matching destination.

**When the specific offering is unfamiliar:** run [Offering Discovery 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) to establish product facts, buyer motivations, meaningful features/benefits, real drawbacks, proof, price/assurances and the approved claim ledger before generating creative. Consume its [offering dossier](../templates/offering-dossier-and-marketing-plan.md). Keep the creative brief selective rather than cramming the whole dossier into one ad.

**When the brand is unfamiliar or its voice is inconsistent:** first run [Brand discovery → messaging (Workflow 3A)](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), then draw from the approved [brand messaging library](../templates/brand-messaging-library.md) and [voice guide](../references/brand-voice-and-copy-system.md). Copy may vary by buying occasion, but should retain the brand's recognizable perspective, product truth and allowed claims. Do not infer brand personality from category clichés.

**Broader creative handoffs:** Meta/Instagram visual creative is not the only promotion format. Use [Social/Hook 5D](05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md) for platform-native content and reply strategy, [Creator 5E](05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md) for authentic partnership content/permissions, [PR 5F](05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md) for factual editorial materials, and [Google Ads 5G](05g-paid-search-google-ads.md) for intent-first responsive search ads, [RSA assets](../templates/google-search-rsa-and-assets.md), sitelinks and policy/landing consistency. Different channels require different hooks, proof and approval gates.

**Shopify ecommerce creative:** use [Merchandising 3B](03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md) for actual variant materials/photos, [Shopify SEO/Markets 5H](05h-shopify-ecommerce-seo.md) for query/category and localized landing context, and [Shopping/Meta Ads 5I](05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md) to ensure product-set price/stock and catalog labels match creative. No imaginary SKU, free shipping, sale or durability claims.

## A. Construct a concept from a specific decision

Read the [marketing context file](../templates/marketing-context.md) first. Ground concepts in real customer language and source material ([grounding tools](../references/content-grounding-and-production-tools.md)); for multi-channel output, draft one master asset and transform it per platform ([pipeline](../references/social-media-strategy-hooks-and-community.md#master-asset--platform-transformation-pipeline)).

For each concept answer:

1. **Who is making what choice, in what situation?** Buyer, user, recipient and category-entry point (if known).
2. **What do they seek?** Functional progress, aesthetic enjoyment, emotional benefit, social meaning, risk reduction or convenience.
3. **What is their alternative?** Another product, brand, marketplace, existing habit or no purchase.
4. **Why might this product win?** One meaningful feature-to-benefit chain, not a list of generic adjectives.
5. **Why might it lose?** Real objection, tradeoff, inconvenience, low trust or lack of relevance.
6. **What makes the argument credible?** Verified feature, product demonstration, authentic review, policy, verified comparison or actual offer.
7. **What must a person remember?** Genuine brand identifiers and (where relevant) connection to an occasion.
8. **What will they see after clicking?** PDP/collection that visually and factually fulfills the promise.
9. **How could we tell whether the concept helped?** Primary outcome, test vs control, confounders and limits.

## B. Generate a diverse hypothesis matrix BEFORE copy

| Concept | Buyer situation | Core motivation | Persuasive route | Proof / asset | Main possible objection | Brand cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: style | Self-selected expression | Aesthetic/identity | Show product character | Real detail photos | Too ornate | Actual brand imagery |
| B: gift | Someone selecting for a recipient | Thoughtful choice | Relate product to occasion | Real photos; verified gift services only | Fit/arrival | Actual packaging, if present |
| C: product comparison | Shopping among ring alternatives | Tangible design/style differences | Clear details, selection, value | Actual variants/specs | Quality/price | Recognizable product/brand |

These are **hypotheses for a hypothetical ring seller**; do not represent them as studied audience segments or use imaginary product features.

## B2. Turn the message into an executable creative direction

**Do not stop at a generic "close-up product photo".** Use [creative strategy](../references/creative-strategy.md) to decide what must be *perceived*, [visual direction](../references/visual-direction.md) to specify exactly how to show it, and [platform idioms](../references/platform-idioms-and-voice.md) to choose an appropriate communication form.

1. **Opening comprehension:** What is the first visible object/action? Can a qualified viewer identify the product/benefit at mobile size? Does actual branding appear naturally and early enough?
2. **Visual evidence:** What real detail, scale, use or honest comparison proves the main idea? If assets don't exist, generate a shot request—not false synthetic proof.
3. **Production choices:** Precise framing, lighting, materials/color fidelity, background, props, talent rights, text hierarchy, overlay words and placement.
4. **Video narrative:** If Reels/Stories, write shot-by-shot timeline, voiceover, text, audio, captions and muted-viewing path. See [short-video storytelling](../references/video-storytelling.md) and [storyboard template](../templates/shot-list-and-storyboard.md).
5. **Native communication:** Use relevant gestures/format (try-on, Q&A, macro reveal, unboxing, real creator viewpoint), avoiding forced internet slang or staged customer endorsements.
6. **Adaptations:** Deliberately render Feed 4:5, Reels/Stories 9:16 and carousel 1:1 when relevant. Follow the [Meta placement playbook](../references/meta-placement-playbook.md); preview live UI for crops/safe zones and rendered text behavior.
7. **Rights/enhancements:** Confirm creator identity and paid-media license, music/audio, FTC disclosure where applicable, and whether Advantage+ AI can alter claims, image or destination. See [creator rights](../references/creator-partnerships-and-rights.md).
8. **Review:** Apply [creative QC rubric](../references/creative-quality-control.md) and [scorecard template](../templates/creative-review-scorecard.md); identify exact edits. The decision to run an ad is separate and requires account authorization.

**Output per creative:** A real [direction brief](../templates/creative-direction-brief.md), storyboard or shot list, editable copy and VO, placement matrix, proof/rights/evidence ledger, disclaimer/CTA, preliminary QA and testing hypothesis. See [fully worked Reels example](../examples/reels-creative-direction.md).

## C. Write copy only after concept approval

- **Primary text:** Communicate buyer-relevant context and value early. The beginning must stand alone where previews truncate. Length varies by placement and rendering; do not mistake guideline targets for immutable hard caps.
- **Headline:** A distinct product/benefit/offer/action that works even if Meta displays different text combinations. Avoid duplicating the primary text.
- **Description:** Optional additional reassurance/detail, not essential terms; may not appear on all placements.
- **CTA:** Choose from currently available Meta options appropriate to destination; verify in live setup if publishing.
- **Visual brief:** Show the real product, use readable close-ups or relevant use context, maintain brand assets, avoid making unsubstantiated claims in overlays. Draft a concrete shot list or storyboard.
- **Destination:** Name exact landing page, offer match, variant and critical proof; if unknown, label TODO rather than invent a URL.

**Example A — aesthetic** (generic hypothetical product):

> Primary text: Vintage-inspired ring designs with details worth a closer look. Explore the collection and find a favorite.
>
> Headline: Explore Vintage-Style Rings
>
> Description: Discover the designs
>
> Visual: actual stocked styles in close-up, identifiable brand cue.
>
> Hypothesis: a design-led concept may help customers whose main buying motivation is aesthetic expression.

**Example B — gift**:

> Primary text: Looking for a gift with personality? Explore vintage-inspired ring designs for someone special.
>
> Headline: A Ring Worth Choosing
>
> Description: Browse ring styles
>
> Visual: real product; no imaginary wrapping, deadlines or guarantees.
>
> Hypothesis: a buying-occasion message may better match gift consideration.

## D. Production checks

Use [the creative template](../templates/creative-production-brief.md), [direction brief](../templates/creative-direction-brief.md), [creative scorecard](../templates/creative-review-scorecard.md), [Meta field guidance](../references/meta-fields.md), [persuasion/signaling](../references/persuasion-and-signaling.md), [compliance](../references/compliance.md) and [quality rubric](../references/quality-rubric.md). Verify:

- Distinct **reasons to buy** (not three wordings of the same promise); one main argument per asset.
- Each factual assertion has a source and each motivational assertion is labeled by confidence.
- No generic hype, fabricated proof, false scarcity, offensive targeting or implied sensitive personal attributes.
- Product shown is available, genuine, accurate and fits the link destination.
- Brand is identifiable, particularly in brand-building creative.
- Visual and copy complement rather than contradict; mobile placement previews still required.
- Hook earns relevant attention rather than cheap/unqualified engagement.

## E. Decision gate

Reject drafts that require fake claims, hide meaningful defects, lack a coherent customer reason, cannot be fulfilled, or mismatch the destination. **Handoff:** send approved **drafts only** (including evidence IDs, brand cues, exact creative and destination notes) to [launch QA](07-launch-qa.md); never infer permission to activate campaigns.
