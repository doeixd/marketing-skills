# Workflow 1 — Commercial diagnosis and product selection

**Trigger:** New product/campaign; poor margins; uncertain which product to promote; proposed increase in ad spend.

**Need holistic research for one selected product, service, package or subscription?** Run [Workflow 1A — Offering Discovery → Marketing Plan](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) after or alongside this commercial check. It produces [an offering dossier](../templates/offering-dossier-and-marketing-plan.md) with buyer jobs, alternative choices, features/benefits, actual tradeoffs, positioning, pricing/assurances, a copy bank and recommended marketing actions.

## A. Questions → evidence

| Question | Evidence to inspect | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| What exactly is sold? | SKU, title, photos, stock/variants, category | Choose a specific product or product family rather than vague brand copy |
| Who pays, who uses, who receives? | Customer orders, recipient patterns if reliably documented | Separate self-use, gifting and organizational buying |
| What job does the buyer hire it for? | Reviews, support, interviews; otherwise hypothesis | Determines what proof and creative to collect next |
| Why is this product commercially attractive? | Revenue, gross profit, returns, repeat behavior, stock, seasonal demand | Select candidate hero SKUs, not only best sellers |
| What are the direct transaction economics? | Net realized price, COGS, variable shipping, fees, packaging, returns, discount costs | Calculate allowable acquisition cost; reject loss-making promotions |
| What's the objective and time horizon? | Business goal, customer acquisition vs profit vs future awareness | Determine primary KPI and learning horizon |
| Is there supply and operational capacity? | Stock, lead times, service ability, restrictions | Don't create demand for a product that cannot be fulfilled |

## B. Calculate economic boundaries

Choose a consistent per-order or per-new-customer basis. For an illustrative **single order**:

```
realized net revenue = price after discounts, excluding sales tax and pass-through charges
pre-ad contribution = realized net revenue - COGS - payment fees - fulfillment/packaging
                      - merchant-paid shipping - expected returns/refunds
                      - other per-order variable cost
break-even acquisition cost = pre-ad contribution
break-even ROAS = realized net revenue / pre-ad contribution  (when contribution > 0)
contribution after ads = pre-ad contribution - attributable acquisition cost
```

Example: $50 net order revenue, $30 total variable cost => $20 pre-ad contribution; break-even **CPA** $20 and break-even **revenue ROAS** 2.5. A target to actually earn incremental profit must be **more conservative** than the break-even threshold. Adjust for fixed costs, repeat purchases, and customer value only when evidence and attribution support it. Don't compare gross sales with net-margin costs.

For multi-product orders and new-customer CAC, use appropriate unit-level or cohort-level profitability rather than mixing definitions. Ads-platform 'purchase' value may differ from realized revenue after discounts, returns, tax and refunds.

## C. Produce a commercial brief

Use [the operating brief](../templates/operating-brief.md). Document named hero product, verified claims, category, objective, economic constraints, capacity, and unresolved assumptions.

## D. Decision gate

- **Proceed to research/positioning** when the product and goal are clear and no critical blocker is hidden.
- **Investigate economics** before paid launch when direct costs, margin or returns are unknown.
- **Fix operations** when stock, fulfillment or product defects make promotion irresponsible.
- **Don't automatically exclude new products:** mark forecast economics and uncertainty; plan a bounded learning test only if authorized.

**Handoff:** product + financial guardrails → [buyer discovery](02-buyer-discovery.md). See [product value/pricing](../references/product-value-and-pricing.md).
