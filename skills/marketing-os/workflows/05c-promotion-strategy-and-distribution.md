# Workflow 5C — Integrated promotion: attention → credibility → qualified demand

**Trigger:** Promote/launch a brand, product/service, event, announcement, collaboration or campaign across organic, earned, owned and paid channels; "how do we get the word out?"; low reach with adequate product-market fit; assemble a campaign.

**Place:** Optional specialist stage under [Stage 5](05-channel-and-campaign.md). Read [promotion fundamentals](../references/promotion-fundamentals-and-integrated-distribution.md), [paid amplification](../references/paid-social-amplification-and-promotion-measurement.md) and [promotion campaign brief](../templates/promotion-campaign-brief.md). Pull brand/voice from [3A](03a-brand-discovery-and-messaging.md), offering facts from [1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md), buyer occasions from [2](02-buyer-discovery.md). Route to [social 5D](05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md), [creators 5E](05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md), [PR 5F](05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md), [creative 6](06-creative-production.md), and [measurement 8](08-measure-diagnose-experiment.md) as needed.

## 0. Define promotional job and readiness

Ask **"Whose awareness, understanding, trust or action are we changing—and why now?"** Scope market, language, buyer occasion, budget, dates, product availability, owner and customer service capacity, approved brand voice/claims, actual event/news/offer, existing audience and current channel results. Brand knowledge is useful; it is not a reason to skip checking product facts.

Decide primary goal: prospective-buyer memory; new information/announcement; category education; proof/consideration; acquisition; partner relationships; loyalty/re-activation. Define one primary business/brand effect, 1–3 learning questions and guardrails. If checkout/product/claim safety is broken, route to [offer/CRO](04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md) first.

## 1. Map buyers and attention environments

For every candidate group record `buyer situation | interest | trusted sources | where they spend attention | what they already know | objections | actual evidence/confidence`. Identify buyer vs user/recipient and journalist/creator intermediary separately. Observe existing communities and customer search behavior, not fabricated demographic preferences.

## 2. Organize a coherent promotion thesis

From brand/offering evidence select:
- One **core message** and verified reason-to-believe;
- One intended memory cue (brand/product/category);
- 2–4 **meaningfully different** story territories: demonstration, comparison, identity, how-to, partner story, genuine news, research, community experience;
- Specific supporting assets and owner-approved claims;
- Accurate destination page and next step.

Answer: why would somebody **notice, care, believe, share or act**? Do not equate a clickbait hook with persuasion. Distinguish editorial newsworthiness from a standard commercial promotion.

## 3. PESO channel plan

| Media | Key decision | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **Owned** | What actual source-of-truth content and conversion destination do we control? | Landing/PDP, helpful guide, FAQ, email, newsroom, media kit |
| **Shared/organic** | Which channel conventions and communities support this buyer job? | Platform-native posts, content calendar, real replies |
| **Earned** | Is there a genuinely relevant story/expert/collaboration worthy of independent attention? | Newsworthiness score, targeted pitches, relationships |
| **Paid** | Is there sufficient margin, rights, budget and measurement to amplify? | Ad concepts, paid partnerships, reach or activation plan |

Rank each by buyer relevance, unique evidence, realistic access, resources, risk, expected business effect and measurability. Choose *fewer* strong channels if the team cannot maintain more. Don't automatically buy a wire, run all platforms or appoint an influencer.

## 4. Plan content and distribution, not only production

Create `asset ID | idea/proof | hook/format | platform/owner | timeline | primary CTA | version | rights | disclosure | URL/UTM | KPI | approval`. Plan one original, valuable source → multiple *adapted* derivatives. Set community moderation and PR/crisis escalation owners. Leave enough time to verify facts, contracts, recordings and approvals.

Sample sequence, adapt to goal:
- **Prepare:** true offer, FAQ/destination, visuals, outreach contacts, measurement.
- **Seed:** genuine teaser only if truthful, partner and journalist conversations where appropriate, organic educational content.
- **Launch:** core asset + on-channel distribution + opted-in owned channels + applicable paid support.
- **Sustain:** Q&A, proof, behind-the-scenes, creator follow-up, partner crosspost, support.
- **Review:** results vs job, update message, fix funnel/creative/partner fit; no guaranteed virality.

## 5. Select paid vs earned vs partner actions

- If paid: check unit contribution, demand, target, creative/rights, ad approval, landing, audience overlap, holdout/experiment possibilities. See [paid reference](../references/paid-social-amplification-and-promotion-measurement.md).
- If creator: specify **paid/gifted/organic** relationship, disclosure, contract, editing/paid-use license and actual audience fit. See [5E](05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md).
- If PR: perform newsworthiness gate and choose direct editorial pitch vs owned release vs wire using real cost/coverage evidence. See [5F](05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md).
- If organic: meet audience's native format and community norms; avoid forced slang, fake personas or blind cadence. See [5D](05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md).

## 6. Measurement and decision

Use AMEC distinction: **activity → outputs/exposure → out-takes (attention/understanding) → outcomes (trust/behavior) → impact (business)**. Record awareness and commercial goals separately, baseline/time horizon, channel definitions and source. Brand lift, quality traffic, qualified leads, purchases, contribution, cancellations, refunds and real engagement quality matter more than raw "potential reach" or likes.

Deliver **ranked channel mix, assets, outreach/partner plan, paid budget gates, calendar, risks, owner approvals, KPI/learning plan**. Recommend a better offer, different audience, useful content or no campaign when promotion isn't the constraint.

**Hard gate:** no actual publishing, spending, cold outreach, paid distribution, press releases or creator commitments without explicit user authorization. No fake scarcity/testimonials or unverified news.
