# Workflow 5E — Creator / micro-influencer / partner discovery → outreach → production → measurement

**Trigger:** Find or evaluate micro/nano creators, influencer or UGC campaign, affiliate/referral partnership, co-marketing, podcast/newsletter collaboration, ambassador program, local partnership, paid creator amplification.

**Place:** Optional [Promotion 5C](05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md) sub-workflow. Read [creator and collaboration research](../references/influencers-collaborations-and-community-partnerships.md), [existing creator rights guide](../references/creator-partnerships-and-rights.md), [FTC guides](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers), and [creator brief](../templates/creator-and-collaboration-brief.md).

## 0. Define job and partnership type

Decide: trusted explanation, category demonstration, content production, access to a relevant community, referrals, actual product research, event education or paid reach. Do not ask an influencer to manufacture objective validation. Distinguish **earned mention, gifting, fee-for-content, paid partnership/whitelisting, affiliate, co-branded asset, paid sponsorship**. The difference determines contract, disclosure, rights and measurement.

Check offering and brand truth, geographic delivery/eligibility, asset/product access, profit/capacity, actual budget and approvals.

## 1. Discover and shortlist relevant partners

Start with who buyers genuinely trust or follow in the **specific situation**: category creators, product testers, stylists, teachers, credible specialists, community organizers, retailers and aligned brands. Discover using actual platform/topic/community, official creator marketplaces, legitimate public profiles, referral suggestions and authorized contact info.

Create a verified candidate ledger: `creator/partner | platform | niche/audience evidence | genuine samples | brand fit | authenticity red flags | contact route | location suitability | rights/cost hypothesis | status`. No invented account statistics or email addresses.

Score **audience relevance, credibility, craft, actual engagement quality, brand safety, demonstrable fit, cost/rights**, but identify unknowns. A "micro-influencer" isn't a universally superior conversion channel; follower boundaries and empirical results vary across platforms and studies.

## 2. Choose a collaboration concept

For each candidate answer:
- What **actual value** does the creator's audience receive?
- Why is this creator/partner personally equipped to communicate that value?
- What is the source/demonstration and what product limitation should be acknowledged?
- Would it sound like a genuine creator voice? What must remain unscripted?
- What assets, rights and genuine disclosures are needed?
- How does audience action reach an honest destination and remain attributable with caveats?

Candidate formats: style try-on, product demo, useful tutorial, independent comparison, Q&A, behind-the-scenes with authorized team, co-hosted class/event, podcast, co-branded buyer guide, real unboxing. Avoid fake reviews or paid-but-undisclosed editorial.

## 3. Build deal/rights/disclosure brief before outreach

Use [creator and collaboration brief](../templates/creator-and-collaboration-brief.md). Include exact deliverables, production/approval timeline, payment/free-product/affiliate terms, creative freedoms, actual claim and policy requirements, **organic repost vs paid ad usage** (different rights), term/territory/exclusivity, music/photo/likeness licensing, brand safety, analytics permissions, cancellation and revision/approval boundaries.

FTC: disclose **material connections** including gifted products and personal/employment relationships prominently in applicable content; in-video/on-Story as appropriate, not buried behind "more"; platform paid partnership labels may not suffice. Never require a positive opinion. Check country-specific rules and current platform partnership permissions.

For Meta Partnership Ads or TikTok Spark Ads, verify in-account authorization and duration; do not treat a Collab tag or organic creator post as indefinite paid-media permission. Record rights expiration and actual music license for paid reuse.

## 4. Prepare respectful personalized outreach — user approval before sending

Draft a concise outreach pitch referencing **real content/topic**, why the idea is useful to *their audience*, proposed concept/fee or ask, deliverables and editorial independence. Ask for media kit/audience metrics and rates where needed; avoid faked familiarity, automated mass DMing and personal-data harvesting.

Offer fair compensation and clear terms; gifting never creates entitlement to a post or positive review. A publisher/editorial outlet may choose independently whether to cover the offering.

## 5. Execute and review

Only after authorized: negotiate, record agreement, ship/provide actual offering, review product facts, disclosures, talent/music/usage, ensure creator may accurately express own experience, verify copy/visual against brand, live destination and policy, publish or sponsor within approved scope.

## 6. Measure whole campaign

Track costs: creator fee, gift/fulfillment, production, rights, paid boost, management, returns. Review output quality, actual disclosed posts, true audience reach, engaged relevant conversations, opted-in sessions, qualified purchases/leads, contribution and any brand effect. Codes/UTMs are partial attribution; compare baseline or experimental controls if feasible, account for code leakage and organic effects.

Record `creator | audience hypothesis | promised value | paid/earned classification | assets | rights end date | real costs | results + uncertainty | renew/change/stop`.

**Stop:** no verified partner identity, coerced testimonials, unclear rights/disclosures, contract/brand conflict, suspicious fake engagement, unsupported claims, insufficient margin, missing sign-off.
