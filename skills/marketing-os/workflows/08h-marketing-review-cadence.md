# Workflow 8H — Marketing review cadence: weekly, monthly and quarterly decisions

**Trigger:** "Set up a weekly marketing review", "what should I look at each week?", recurring reporting, deciding what to scale or cut, a results recap, or any ongoing marketing with no regular decision routine.

**Place:** The operating rhythm on top of [8A analytics](08a-marketing-analytics-and-measurement.md), [8B incrementality](08b-attribution-incrementality-and-experiments.md) and [8C customer analytics](08c-customer-segmentation-rfm-and-clv.md). It uses the [performance review template](../templates/marketing-campaign-performance-review.md), the [KPI dictionary](../templates/marketing-measurement-and-kpi-dictionary.md), the [media plan](../templates/media-plan-and-budget.md) and the [experiment decision log](../templates/experiment-decision-log.md). Dashboards describe; this routine decides.

## 1. Set it up once

- **KPI tree:** one business outcome at the top (contribution profit, new customers, qualified pipeline), the 3–6 drivers under it, and one owner and one source of truth for each. Platform-reported numbers sit beside, never above, store or CRM numbers.
- **Targets and ranges:** from the media plan's low/base/high forecast, so "off plan" has a definition.
- **Decision rules:** written in advance (see the media plan): minimum data before judging, thresholds for scale / hold / cut, maximum change per step, who approves.

## 2. Weekly review (about 30 minutes)

1. **Data check first:** tracking working, events not duplicated, spend and orders reconcile, anything launched or broken this week.
2. **Business result:** orders, revenue, contribution, new customers or pipeline vs plan and vs the same week a year ago; blended CAC/MER.
3. **Spend pacing:** planned vs actual by channel.
4. **Exceptions only:** what moved outside its expected range, and the two or three most likely causes, including non-marketing ones (stock, price, season, site, competitors).
5. **Tests:** status of running experiments; no peeking decisions before the planned end.
6. **Qualitative:** comment themes, support and sales feedback, reviews, recordings.
7. **Decisions:** at most a few, each with owner and date, recorded in the decision log.

Weekly numbers are noisy. Don't reallocate budget on one week unless something is broken.

## 3. Monthly review

Channel performance against roles and marginal CAC; cohort retention and repeat purchase; creative and message learnings (what was learned about buyers, not just which ad won); funnel conversion by step and device; budget reallocation under the pre-agreed rules; next month's test plan; context-file updates (new verified facts, stale rows).

## 4. Quarterly review

Is the strategy still right: positioning and offer evidence, channel mix and dependency risk, brand vs activation balance, incrementality results, CAC payback and CLV trends, capacity and stock, budget reset for the next quarter, and what to stop doing.

## 5. Report format

For any audience, lead with: **what happened → why we think so (and how sure) → what we decided → what we need**. Separate `verified` results from interpretation; state attribution caveats once, plainly; never present platform-attributed revenue as incremental; include what didn't work.

**Stop conditions:** no trustworthy source of truth for orders or leads (fix measurement first via [8A](08a-marketing-analytics-and-measurement.md)/[8E](08e-event-instrumentation-and-conversion-qa.md)); nobody with authority to act on decisions attends.
