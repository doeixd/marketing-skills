# Workflow 5J — Shopify seasonal/offer campaigns and merchandise-led promotion

**Trigger:** Shopify seasonal sale, product launch, gift guide, Black Friday/Cyber Monday, holiday campaign, clearance, bundle, quantity offer, shipping threshold, limited drop, ecommerce content/paid promotion calendar, or "how should we promote these products?"

**Place:** Stage 5 specialist, connects [Store 1B](01b-shopify-ecommerce-diagnosis.md), [Catalog 3B](03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md), [Promotion 5C](05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md), [Ads 5I](05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md), [Lifecycle 9A](09a-shopify-lifecycle-retention.md) and [CRO 4B](04b-shopify-ecommerce-cro.md). Read [Shopify promotion research](../references/shopify-promotions-bundles-and-seasonal-campaigns.md), use [promotion plan](../templates/shopify-ecommerce-promotion-brief.md).

## 0. Define actual buyer occasion and business goal

Ask which products/collections, why now, target buyers, channel, actual event/season, discounts allowed, stock, real margin, delivery cutoffs, terms, dates, countries and owner. A relevant occasion is not automatically an urgency claim. Choose **full-price discovery, new product, gifting, complement/bundle, return customer, clearance, profitable inventory movement** before inventing a sale.

## 1. Calculate promotion viability

For each proposed offer estimate SKU/variant contribution **before and after** discount, shipping subsidy, packaging, payment fees, returns and marketing/creator costs. Compare likely full-price cannibalization and true availability. Avoid saying a "$20 off" promotion is profitable from a conversion rate forecast alone. If data are missing, present scenarios and exact requested inputs.

## 2. Shopify discount, bundle and channel QA

Inspect automatic vs code discount, applicable product/collection/segment, markets, times, combinability, quantity limits and shipping/tax behavior. Shopify discount classes and eligibility differ by store plan; bundle price and inventory constraints must be checked. Test realistic carts with multiple offers, excluded SKUs, gift cards, multi-currency, shipping threshold and stock. No silent live discount creation.

## 3. Create persuasive merchandising and creative

Build a useful collection/product landing experience with real products in stock; verified price/benefit/proof, gift/fit/returns guidance, actual shipping dates and clear eligibility. Create `campaign premise → buyer question → hero/product selection → authentic proof → creative angle/hooks → truthful offer CTA → linked landing`. Use [Social 5D](05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md), [Meta Creative 6](06-creative-production.md), [Google Ads 5G](05g-paid-search-google-ads.md), [PR/creators 5E–5F](05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md) only when relevant. Promotional copy must not imply discounts/availability beyond current terms.

## 4. Schedule and distribute

Plan prelaunch preparation, relevant launch, useful reminders without false urgency, customer support capacity, postcampaign service and authentic postmortem. Coordinate Shopify site banner/collection/policy, Search/Shopping/Meta product feeds, email/SMS eligible segments, partners and organic posts; define exactly who owns each asset/approval and source-of-truth product facts.

## 5. Measure *incremental* qualified commerce

Report campaign cost, net sales/discounts, product mix, realized contribution, fulfillment, refunds, new buyer quality, existing buyer cannibalization, stockout complaints and customer service burden. Media/platform attribution isn't proof of additional sales. A holdout/control can help if feasible; otherwise label observational differences.

**Deliver:** [Shopify ecommerce promotion brief](../templates/shopify-ecommerce-promotion-brief.md), margin/stacking analysis, truthful creative and landing specs, owner approvals, launch QA, campaign calendar, results/learning review.

**Hard gates:** no made-up strike-through prices, perpetual fake countdowns, unverified stock/ship promises, customer suppression violations, or automatic ads/discount activation without explicit authorization.
