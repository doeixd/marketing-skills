# Workflow 5 — Marketing channel and campaign planning

**Trigger:** Product, buyer, positioning and purchase experience are understood well enough to acquire attention; team needs a channel plan rather than isolated ads.

**Product/service-specific plan:** Use [Offering Discovery 1A](01a-offering-discovery-and-marketing-plan.md) to establish whom the offering serves, what purchase occasions exist, what evidence and creative assets are available, what blockers remain and how the offer economics constrain spend. Its [offering dossier](../templates/offering-dossier-and-marketing-plan.md) supplies input here. Choose channels by buyer intent and feasibility rather than defaulting to Meta.

**Organic discovery extension:** [SEO 5A](05a-seo-and-organic-search.md) maps real search intent to findable/helpful pages and catalog/merchant data; [AEO 5B](05b-aeo-ai-search-visibility.md) investigates AI-answer source quality and citation observability. Neither guarantees rankings or citations. For landing-page failures see [CRO 4A](04a-conversion-rate-optimization.md).

**Promotion and demand-capture extensions:** Choose [Promotion 5C](05c-promotion-strategy-and-distribution.md) when coordinating paid/earned/shared/owned work, [Social 5D](05d-social-content-hooks-and-community.md) for hooks/content/community, [Creator 5E](05e-creators-influencers-and-collaborations.md) for micro-influencers/affiliates/UGC and proper rights, [PR 5F](05f-pr-earned-media-and-press-releases.md) for genuine news and editorial outreach, and [Paid Search 5G](05g-paid-search-google-ads.md) when buyers actively search for a solution/product. Google Search must use correct conversion goals, match and negative keyword policies, real landing evidence and budget authorization. See [promotion plan](../templates/promotion-campaign-brief.md) and [Google Ads blueprint](../templates/google-ads-search-campaign-blueprint.md).

**Shopify ecommerce extension:** [SEO/Markets 5H](05h-shopify-ecommerce-seo.md) connects organic search with real collections/products; [Shopping/Meta Ads 5I](05i-shopify-shopping-ads-and-tracking.md) handles product feed eligibility, pixel consent, SKU economics and campaign QA. Both start from an accurate Shopify catalog, [Store Diagnosis 1B](01b-shopify-ecommerce-diagnosis.md) and [Merchandising 3B](03b-shopify-catalog-and-merchandising.md). Do not increase traffic until buyers can complete a real purchase.

**Retention and permissioned communications:** when the customer is already in a legitimate relationship or has opted into relevant marketing, [Email 9B](09b-email-marketing-campaigns-and-copy.md) and [SMS 9C](09c-sms-text-and-messaging-marketing.md) may be more appropriate than buying another impression. Before recommending them verify channel-specific legal eligibility, product value and sender/flow readiness via [9D](09d-cross-channel-lifecycle-and-segmentation.md)/[9E](09e-email-deliverability-and-list-health.md); measure incremental quality with [8A](08a-marketing-analytics-and-measurement.md)/[8B](08b-attribution-incrementality-and-experiments.md).

## A. Choose the commercial job before the channel

| Commercial job | Buyer state / business question | Candidate channels or tactics | Primary evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Become remembered for buying occasions | Many future buyers aren't currently shopping | Consistent organic content, video, creator work, paid reach where appropriate | Brand recognition, category associations, reach, longer-term business response |
| Capture existing demand | Buyer already wants category/product | Search, product feeds, SEO, retail discovery, category pages | Qualified product visits, contribution from orders |
| Win comparison / convert interest | Buyer is evaluating an offering | Meta product education, proof-led ads, product-page improvements, email with permission | Purchases, allowable CPA, contribution, relevant experiments |
| Grow customer value | Real potential for compatible future purchase | Postpurchase email, cross-sell, replenishment when valid, loyalty/service | Repeat contribution, return rate, customer satisfaction |
| Learn if new positioning has value | Demand/fit not established | Small bounded creative, survey, interviews, controlled tests | Quality of evidence; commercial signals, not just CTR |

Don't claim Meta cannot reach high intent or that email inherently retains everyone; channels can play multiple roles. Choose based on where the customer can be reached, what the business can realistically supply, and what can be measured.

Turn the chosen channel roles into money and expected results with the [media plan 5P](05p-media-plan-and-budget-allocation.md). Beyond Meta and Google Search see [5L](05l-paid-social-and-video-platforms.md); marketplaces [5M](05m-marketplace-listings-and-ads.md); local [5N](05n-local-marketing-and-reviews.md); B2B/SaaS [5O](05o-b2b-saas-demand-generation.md).

## B. Essential campaign questions

1. Is the objective **immediate activation, brand memory, or both**? If both, specify distinct outputs and measurement expectations.
2. What buying occasion/CEP and which product or offer will this campaign represent?
3. What audience/reach assumptions are permissible and evidence-based? Never assume protected personal attributes or medical/financial vulnerabilities.
4. Why is this channel suitable relative to alternatives, and what role should other channels play?
5. What creative formats and landing pages exist? What must be produced?
6. What does each campaign need to teach us? Choose a product, audience occasion, positioning or offer **hypothesis**, not an arbitrary number of ads.
7. What are the **spend boundaries**, minimum economic targets, inventory constraints, and stop conditions? Get authorization for real spend.
8. How will we reconcile platform reports with actual paid orders, refunds, repeat behavior, and broader brand outcomes?

## C. Build an actionable campaign plan

Record `campaign ID | commercial job | product | buying occasion | positioning/offer | channel | asset requirements | destination | expected customer action | primary KPI | countermetric | learning hypothesis | budget authorization | owner`.

**Example campaign roles** (illustrative, not platform setup recommendations):

- **A. Occasion memory:** build recognition for a gift-shopping context with repeated, real brand cues; measure appropriate reach/brand outcomes and downstream sales directional response.
- **B. Product preference:** explain distinct designs, verified quality, or service through proof-rich video or carousel to people interested in the category.
- **C. Conversion:** show actual product and price/value, answer the dominant objection, make it easy to reach the right PDP; judge on net contribution and orders.
- **D. Lifecycle:** respond to consented existing customers with relevant follow-ups, not generic repeated discounts.

## D. Handoff + gate

Proceed to [creative production](06-creative-production.md) when each campaign has one primary job, prioritized buyer occasion, real offer, approved proof and brand cues, suitable destination, tracking plan and economic guardrails. If none exists, return to [buyer discovery](02-buyer-discovery.md) or [offer readiness](04-offer-and-conversion.md).

For Meta-specific setup, consult current [Meta Blueprint Ads Manager learning](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/collection/507792-meta-ads-manager-learning), [creative training](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/211553-ads-manager-visual-ads-course), and [marketing effectiveness](../references/marketing-effectiveness.md). A plan does **not** confer permission to publish, contact customers, or spend money.
