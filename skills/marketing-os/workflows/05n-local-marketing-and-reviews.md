# Workflow 5N — Local marketing: Google Business Profile, local search, reviews and bookings

**Trigger:** A local or service-area business (shop, restaurant, clinic, trades, studio, agency with local clients); Google Business Profile, Google Maps ranking, "near me" searches, local SEO, getting more calls or bookings, Local Services Ads, or review requests for a physical business.

**Place:** Optional specialist in [Stage 5](05-channel-and-campaign.md); builds on [SEO 5A](05a-seo-and-organic-search.md) and [Google Ads 5G](05g-paid-search-google-ads.md). Review and reputation policy is in [9F](09f-advocacy-reviews-referrals-and-reputation.md). Items marked *(reported)* are from third-party sources in 2026; confirm in Google's Business Profile Help.

## 1. Define the service area and the conversion

Record real locations, service radius, hours, services with prices or ranges, capacity (how many more jobs or bookings can be handled), average job value and margin, and the conversion that matters: call, booking, form, direction request or walk-in. Advertising beyond capacity or outside the service area wastes money.

## 2. Make the profile complete and true

Google Business Profile is usually the highest-leverage asset.

- Correct primary and secondary categories, name exactly as used in the real world (no keyword stuffing), address or hidden address for service-area businesses, phone, hours including holidays, services and products, attributes, booking link.
- Real, recent photos of premises, team, work and products.
- Regular posts and offers that are actually valid.
- *(Reported)* Google is retiring the Q&A section in favor of an AI "Ask Maps" answer built from the profile's fields and reviews. Put the answers customers need into services, attributes, descriptions and the website FAQ rather than relying on Q&A.
- One profile per real location; no virtual offices or fake addresses (suspension risk).
- Claim and align the same facts on Apple Business Connect, Bing Places and the directories that matter in the category.

## 3. Make the website back it up

A page per real location or service with unique, useful content (not city-name doorway pages); name, address and phone consistent with the profile; `LocalBusiness` structured data that matches visible content; click-to-call, booking and directions above the fold on mobile; prices, service area and proof (photos of real work, licences, insurance, guarantees).

## 4. Earn reviews the permitted way

- Ask **every** customer shortly after service, with a direct link or QR code. Never ask only happy customers first (review gating), offer incentives for Google reviews, set staff quotas, use an on-premises review kiosk, or tell customers what to write. *(Reported)* Google made these explicit violations in April 2026; they also conflict with the FTC reviews rule.
- Yelp discourages asking for reviews at all; follow each platform's own policy.
- Reply to all reviews, positive and negative, factually and without revealing customer details. See the response guide in the [reviews reference](../references/reviews-referrals-loyalty-and-reputation.md).

## 5. Add paid local demand capture if capacity allows

- **Search ads** on service + location terms with tight location targeting (presence, not interest), call and location assets, ad schedule matching answering hours.
- **Local Services Ads** (pay per lead, with verification/licensing checks) for eligible categories. *(Reported)* Google is moving LSA management into Google Ads; check the current setup.
- Track calls with call reporting or a tracking number set as the profile's primary number while keeping the real number as an additional one, so listings stay consistent.

## 6. Measure outcomes, not rank

Profile interactions (calls, direction requests, bookings), tracked calls and forms, booked jobs and revenue by source, review count/rating/recency, cost per booked job. Map rank varies by the searcher's location, so treat rank trackers as directional. Ask "how did you hear about us?" at intake and record it.

**Stop conditions:** unverified or suspended profile; business not eligible for a profile at that address; no capacity to answer leads promptly (fix response time before buying leads).

**Sources:** [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/), [Google guidelines for representing your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177), [Google Maps user-contributed content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114); Q&A retirement per [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-maps-qa-feature-ask-40594.html); review-policy update per [Yext](https://www.yext.com/blog/google-april-updates-reviews-local-search-ads).
